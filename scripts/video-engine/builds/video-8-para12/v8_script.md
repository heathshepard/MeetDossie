**Source:** TREC 20-19 ¶12.B, page 7 of 12. The operative sentence is set in
**bold on the form itself** and sits directly above the 12B(1)/12B(2) boxes:
*"The contributions under 12B(1) and 12B(2) below shall be applied to and shall
not change the parties' obligations to pay compensation pursuant to those
agreements."* Verified by `pdftotext -layout` against
`scripts/trec-forms/20-19.pdf`; the form shown on screen is the blank
promulgated PDF, enforced by `scripts/video-engine/assert-blessed-pdf.js`.

**Cover hook text:** `SELLER PAYS 2%. YOU'RE OWED 3%.`

**Stake:** The 1% the seller's contribution does not cover — $4,000 on a $400,000 sale — is money the agent either collects from a blindsided buyer at the closing table or loses.

**Dropped lines:** Three lines were cut for runtime, from a 56s read down to the 34s TikTok ceiling. (1) "which is the worst place in the world for that conversation to happen" — emotional colour on top of the stake, which the line "at the closing table" already carries. (2) "12B just moves money toward it." — restates the two sentences immediately before it. (3) "It does not change it." — Heath's plain-English paraphrase, cut because the very next line quotes the form's own words, "shall not change," and the line after that spells out the consequence in dollars. Nothing from the hook, the citation, the stake, the re-hook or the CTA was cut, and the closing benefit clause is delivered in full.

### SCRIPT

`[HOOK]`

The seller agrees to contribute two percent toward your buyer's broker compensation. It's in the contract, paragraph 12B.

`[SETUP]`

Your buyer's rep agreement says three percent, so you're covered, right?

`[REVEAL]`

Read the sentence right above those boxes. The contribution gets applied to what your client already agreed to pay you.

`[QUOTE]`

Those are the contract's words, shall not change.

`[PAYOFF]`

So the seller's two percent comes in, and your buyer still owes you the other one percent at closing.

`[STAKE]`

And if your buyer didn't know that was coming, you're having that conversation at the closing table.

`[REHOOK]`

The contract isn't deciding what you get paid, the buyer's rep agreement decides that.

`[CTA]`

`[KEYWORD]`

Comment paid, and I'll send you a plain English breakdown of paragraph 12, box by box.
