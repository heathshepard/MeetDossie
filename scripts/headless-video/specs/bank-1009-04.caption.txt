Nobody moves to the Hill Country for a spreadsheet. This is Boerne, Texas: Cibolo Creek through the middle of town, bald cypress that go copper in the fall, a bandstand on the Main Plaza, ducks that own the pond next to it, limestone storefronts on Main Street, and the Hill Country ridge right behind the rooftops. Close enough to San Antonio to work there, far enough that it doesn't feel like it. Drive through once, park on Main Street, walk to the creek, then try to stop thinking about it. If you've already made that drive, comment BOERNE and tell me the first thing that got you.
