One deadline in the Texas contract doesn't start on the effective date. TREC 20-19 paragraph 6.B: within 20 days after the Title Company RECEIVES a copy of the contract, the seller shall furnish the buyer a title commitment. If nobody sends the executed contract to title, that clock never started. Late? The time extends automatically, up to 15 more days or 3 days before closing, whichever is earlier. Miss that too and the buyer may terminate and the earnest money goes back to the buyer. Send the contract to title the day it's executed and note the day title received it. Comment TITLE for my checklist of every deadline in the contract.
