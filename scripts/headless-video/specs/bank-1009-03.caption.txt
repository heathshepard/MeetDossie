The three-day rule, read wrong both ways. TREC 20-19 paragraph 5.A: within 3 days after the effective date the buyer must deliver the earnest money and the option fee -- calendar days, not business days. Sign Thursday and day 3 is Sunday. Paragraph 5.A(2) rolls a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday deadline to the next day that isn't one -- but that extension appears exactly twice in the whole contract, both inside 5.A, and it covers only the earnest money, the option fee and additional earnest money. The 5.B option notice does not roll: due by 5:00 p.m. on the date specified, Saturday included. Late option fee? 5.D says the buyer loses the unrestricted right to terminate.
