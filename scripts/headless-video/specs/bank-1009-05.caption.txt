My transaction coordinator went dark in the middle of a deal. That's when I found out how much of the file sat on one person -- the dates, the contacts, the status of every notice. The contract didn't care: TREC 20-19 paragraph 5.E says time is of the essence for that paragraph and strict compliance is required, and none of the paragraph 5 clocks pause because the person tracking them disappeared. The lesson isn't "hire a better coordinator." It's that no deadline in your file should have exactly one person who knows about it.
