## Cross-cutting notes for review

1. **Date discrepancies are a pattern, not an error on my part.** Every form shows two dates: the
   date printed on the PDF itself (the text revision date) and a separate "Effective Date" listed
   on the form's trec.texas.gov landing page (when the version becomes required for use — usually
   weeks to ~2 months later). I cited both, distinctly labeled, in every guide rather than picking
   one and presenting it as the only date.
2. **57-0 (Non-Realty Items Addendum) has the one real anomaly**: landing page says effective
   9/3/2025, but the PDF text itself is dated 10-10-11 with no visible revision. I flagged this
   directly in the guide body rather than silently picking a date — worth a manual TREC-site check
   before this guide ships, in case there's a newer PDF I didn't land on.
3. **Nothing in any of the 7 guides cites the existing 10 guides' form numbers or facts.** Where
   overlap exists (e.g., references to the option period, Paragraph 5, the contract's Effective
   Date), those are restated only insofar as they appear directly on the new form's own text
   (e.g., 38-8's own reference to "Paragraph 5 of the contract"), not pulled from the old guides.
4. **No dollar figures, percentages, or "typical" ranges were invented anywhere.** Every dollar
   line in these forms (financing amounts, HOA fee caps, option fee extensions, additional-sum
   consideration) is a blank on the actual TREC form, and every guide describes it as
   "negotiated"/"filled in by the parties" rather than supplying an assumed number.
