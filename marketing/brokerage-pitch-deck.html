Under The Hood

What she actually does, file by file.

TREC Deadline Engine
Option, financing, title, survey, closing — every critical date auto-calculated the moment a contract is uploaded, cited to the paragraph.
Contract Scan
Reads parties, price, dates, and addenda from an uploaded TREC contract, then flags missing signatures, initials, and blank required fields.
Auto Follow-Ups
Drafts welcome notes, lender check-ins, title requests, and closing reminders from the agent's own email address. Agent approves with one tap.
Morning Brief
One tap, every morning: what's due today, what's coming up, what needs attention right now — across every active file.
Pipeline View
Every deal at a glance, by stage, by agent. Click into any file for the full timeline and document set.
E-Sign Workflow
Send disclosures and amendments for signature without leaving the file — no separate DocuSign login for the agent to juggle.
Closing Checklist
A standardized, office-wide checklist per file type — the same list every agent works from, every time.
"Hey Dossie" Voice
Voice-first design for the car or a showing — talk to Dossie, she listens and takes action. Coming soon.
