How Dossie Handles Contract Scanning

The 8-second scan, mid-flight — needs-review flags on the right.

Drop in a signed TREC contract — PDF or photo — and Dossie reads the whole thing: price, dates, parties, addenda, every field. No retyping, no re-keying deal terms from a scanned PDF into a spreadsheet.

1. Upload

Drag in the executed contract. Any TREC form, any scan quality — a phone photo works as well as a clean PDF export.

2. Auto-fill

Dossie extracts every field into the dossier automatically: sales price, effective date, option fee, financing type, parties, every addendum attached.

3. Flag, don't guess

Missing signatures, missing initials, and blank required fields get marked Needs review — Dossie never assumes a page is complete just because it's present.

Frequently asked

What file types does Dossie's contract scan accept? +
PDF and common image formats (JPG, PNG) — including phone photos of a signed contract. Scan quality doesn't need to be perfect.
What does Dossie flag as "Needs review"? +
Missing signatures, missing initials on individual pages, and blank required fields. Dossie surfaces these instead of silently treating the contract as complete.
