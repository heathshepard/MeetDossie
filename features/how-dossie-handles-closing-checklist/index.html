How Dossie Handles the Closing Checklist

The last mile of a deal is where things get missed. Dossie's closing checklist is built for whichever side you're on — buyer or seller — and stays visible until every item is checked.

By Heath Shepard, Texas REALTOR® Updated 2026-08-07
meetdossie.com/workspace

A dossier mid-close — two items verified, two still open.

The days right before closing are when a missed step actually costs something — an unconfirmed payoff, a walkthrough nobody did, keys nobody arranged. Dossie's closing checklist lives in every dossier and shows the exact list for that side of the deal, buyer or seller, so nothing gets confirmed from memory.

1. The right list, automatically

Buyer-side dossiers get buyer tasks — CD reviewed, prorations verified, final walkthrough, repairs confirmed. Seller-side dossiers get seller tasks — CD reviewed, net proceeds matched, payoff confirmed, keys ready.

2. Check items off as they're verified

Click any item to mark it done — it turns green with a strikethrough, so what's left is obvious at a glance.

3. Nothing closes the loop on its own

The checklist doesn't auto-check anything — every item stays open until an agent confirms it, which is the whole point.

Buyer and seller dossiers show different checklists on purpose — a buyer doesn't need "keys ready for delivery," and a seller doesn't need "final walkthrough completed."

Frequently asked

Does the closing checklist look different for buyers and sellers? +
Yes. Buyer-side items include things like final walkthrough and repair verification; seller-side items include net proceeds matching the net sheet and confirming the mortgage payoff. Each dossier only shows the list for its own side.
Does anything on the checklist check itself automatically? +
No — every item is a manual confirmation. Dossie tracks deadlines and flags documents automatically elsewhere, but closing verification is intentionally something the agent confirms by hand.
Where does the closing checklist live? +
Inside every dossier, in its own Closing section — reachable from the dossier's section nav alongside Deadlines, Offers, and Documents.
