Frequently asked

Which milestones generate a shareable card? +
Three: Under Contract, Clear to Close, and Closed. Each has its own headline and subhead, drawn fresh every time a dossier reaches that stage.
Is any private deal information shown on the card? +
No. The card shows only the property's city and state — never buyer or seller names, the street address, or the sale price. It's built to be safe to post publicly.
Where do past milestone cards go after I share them? +
Every card is saved to that dossier's Milestones section automatically, so you can reopen, re-download, or re-share it later without redoing the work.
Can I share the card from my phone? +
Yes — on mobile, the share buttons open your device's native share sheet straight to Instagram or Facebook. On desktop, Dossie downloads the PNG so you can attach it yourself.
