## PARAGRAPH DELTA — 20-18 → 20-19

Agents mid-transaction under 20-18 continue under 20-18; you do not re-paper an executed
contract. Every NEW offer drafted on or after 2026-07-01 must be on 20-19.

**The six material changes:**

### 1. NEW ¶7.I — Seller's Disclosure About Groundwater and Surface Water Rights

**This is the biggest change, and v1 put it in the wrong place entirely.** It is **¶7.I**, in
PROPERTY CONDITION. It is **not** in ¶6.E. It had no analog in 20-18.

Structure — **check one box only**:

- **7.I(1)** Buyer has received the Seller's Water Disclosure.
- **7.I(2)** Buyer has **not** received it. Seller delivers within ____ days after the Effective Date.
- **7.I(3)** Seller is **not required** to deliver it, because **all five** of the following are true:
  (a) Seller not aware of a water well on the Property (**in use or not in use**);
  (b) Seller not aware of a pond, lake, or water tank on the Property;
  (c) Seller not aware of any certificate of adjudication, certified filing, or permit for surface
      water rights administered by TCEQ;
  (d) Seller not aware that any groundwater rights have been severed, sold, or leased in whole or
      in part; and
  (e) the Property receives water **only** from a named City / MUD or other special district /
      WSC / private water supply company (**fill in the blank — naming the provider is part of
      the election**).

> #### The termination right in 7.I(2) OUTLIVES THE OPTION PERIOD. Flag this to every agent.
> If Buyer **never receives** the Water Disclosure, Buyer **"may terminate this contract at any
> time prior to the closing"** and the earnest money is refunded. That is not bounded by the
> Option Period.
> If Seller **does deliver it late**, Buyer may terminate **for any reason within 7 days after
> receipt**, or prior to closing, whichever first occurs — earnest money refunded.
>
> This is structurally identical to the long-standing ¶7.B Seller's Disclosure Notice remedy, and
> it is the same trap: a seller-side failure to deliver leaves a live, post-option exit sitting in
> the contract. On a listing, get the Water Disclosure delivered or get 7.I(3) correctly elected.

Practitioner note: in a platted San Antonio subdivision on SAWS, 7.I(3) takes ten seconds. On Hill
Country acreage with a well, it is a conversation, not a checkbox. Note "**in use or not in use**"
— a capped, forgotten well still defeats 7.I(3)(a).

The referenced standalone form is the **Seller's Water Disclosure published by TREC**. The contract
prints no form number for it (see "Form numbers" caution below).

### 2. Brokerage compensation moved out of ¶12.A(1)(b) and became a two-way ¶12.B

In 20-18, seller contribution toward the buyer's broker sat in **¶12.A(1)(b)**. The redline
strikes it. Also struck: "brokerage fees that Seller has agreed to pay" from ¶12.A(1)(a), and
"brokerage fees that Buyer has agreed to pay" from ¶12.A(2). Old **¶12.A(1)(c)** is renumbered to
**¶12.A(1)(b)** and now reads "an amount not to exceed $____ to be applied to Buyer's Expenses
**other than brokerage compensation or contributions under Paragraph 12B below**."

**New ¶12.B BROKERAGE COMPENSATION** — opens "Brokerage compensation is not set by law and is
fully negotiable," and runs **both directions**:

- **12.B(1)** Seller will pay (check one box only) ☐ $______ or ☐ ______% of the Sales Price
  toward the brokerage compensation owed by **Buyer to Buyer's broker**.
- **12.B(2)** Buyer will pay (check one box only) ☐ $______ or ☐ ______% of the Sales Price
  toward the brokerage compensation owed by **Seller to Seller's broker**.

Each row has an **enabling checkbox plus a $/% sub-choice** — three boxes per row.

Bolded in the form: these contributions "**shall be applied to and shall not change the parties'
obligations to pay compensation pursuant to those agreements.**" A seller contribution is not a
commission agreement — if a buyer-rep agreement says 2.5% and the seller contributes 2%, the client
still owes the balance.

**Old ¶12.B is gone entirely.** 20-18's ¶12.B let a party terminate if an expense exceeded a stated
amount. That termination right is **struck, not relocated**. The new **¶12.C EXPENSE LIMITATION**
is narrower and does something different: if a governmental loan program regulation prohibits Buyer
from paying charges or fees, the ¶12.A(1)(b) concession is applied first to those prohibited
expenses, then to other Buyer's Expenses, but **not** to brokerage compensation or contribution.

### 3. ¶8 renamed; brokers' fees language deleted

20-18 ¶8 was "**BROKERS AND SALES AGENTS**" with an 8.A and an 8.B. 20-19 ¶8 is
"**BROKER OR SALES AGENT DISCLOSURE**", a **single unlettered block**.

- Old 8.A's heading is absorbed into the paragraph title; the disclosure text itself survives
  verbatim.
- **Old 8.B is deleted** — "All obligations of the parties for payment of brokers' fees are
  contained in separate written agreements." That concept now lives in ¶12.B.

What remains is the licensee-as-a-party disclosure, and it is broader than most agents assume: the
broker or sales agent who is a party, **or acting on behalf of** a spouse, parent, child, a business
entity in which they own **more than 10%**, or a trust for which they are trustee or of which they
/ their spouse / parent / child is a beneficiary — must notify the other party **in writing before
entering into a contract of sale**.

### 4. ¶6.E lost item (12); the notices moved into ¶22

20-18 ¶6.E TITLE NOTICES ran **(1) through (12)**. 20-19 ¶6.E **ends at (11)**, CERTIFICATE OF MOLD
REMEDIATION. Items (1)-(11) are otherwise unchanged and unrenumbered.

Old **(12) REQUIRED NOTICES** (bracketed/struck in the redline) moved into **¶22**, under the new
"Statutory Disclosures and Notices" group, as the line: "The following utility, water, drainage,
public improvement, and other district notices (list all that have been given or are attached)."
The warning followed it: "**Seller's failure to provide applicable Statutory Disclosures and Notices
may provide Buyer with remedies or rights to terminate this contract.**"

Same trap, new address. There is no longer a 6.E(12) to fill in.

### 5. ¶22 addenda list reorganized into five named groups

No longer one long two-column list. 20-19 groups the checkboxes under **Financial · Leases ·
Additional Tests and Reports · Statutory Disclosures and Notices · Other**. Anyone who finds a box
by its position on the page has to re-learn it; read the group headers.

Also, in the list itself:
- **Added:** "Addendum for Release of Liability on Assumed Loan and/or Restoration of Seller's VA
  Entitlement" and "Non-Realty Items Addendum" (neither appears in 20-18's list).
- **Removed:** "Addendum containing Notice of Obligation to Pay Improvement District Assessment"
  as a standalone checkbox — folded into the district-notices list line described in Change 4.

**The 20-19 ¶22 list names addenda by NAME ONLY. It prints no form numbers anywhere.** See the
form-numbers caution below. Full transcribed list in the ¶22 section further down.

### 6. ¶20 renamed, plus the small cleanups

- **¶20 "FEDERAL REQUIREMENTS" → "GOVERNMENTAL REQUIREMENTS"**, and it is now lettered. The FIRPTA
  / non-foreign-person withholding text becomes **20.A**. **New 20.B:** Seller and Buyer shall
  promptly execute and deliver any statements, documents, and information requested by Escrow Agent
  required by governmental reporting requirements (the form names "the U.S. Treasury or its
  bureaus" as the example).
- **¶21 NOTICES — fax dropped as a delivery method.** 20-18 read "mailed to, hand-delivered at, or
  transmitted by **fax or** electronic transmission." 20-19 reads "mailed to, hand-delivered at,
  **sent by overnight courier to**, or transmitted by electronic transmission." Also now "from one
  party **or their agent** to the other." The notice blocks changed shape too — see ¶21 below.
  (Fax fields still exist in ¶23 attorney blocks and on the page-12 receipts. Only ¶21 lost fax.)
- **¶17 ATTORNEY'S FEES** — "Listing Broker, Other Broker" → "**Seller's broker, Buyer's broker**."
- **Page 11 broker page** — the inter-broker compensation disclosure line ("Pursuant to a previous,
  separate agreement, Listing Broker has agreed to pay Other Broker a fee ☐$___ or ☐___%") is
  **deleted**. Post-NAR-settlement cleanup, finished. Its function is replaced by ¶12.B.
- **¶5** — the defined term "Option Fee" is lowercased to "option fee" throughout; "legal holiday"
  is capitalized to "Legal Holiday" and defined. Cosmetic, but it changes string matching.
- **¶7.B(3)** — "the notice" → "the Seller's Disclosure Notice."

### NOT a change — do not report these as new

- **¶3.B financing checkboxes: still THREE, unchanged.** Third Party Financing Addendum / Loan
  Assumption Addendum / Seller Financing Addendum. Byte-identical in both forms. v1's claim that
  they were "consolidated to 2 boxes" was fabricated.
- **¶10.B SMART DEVICES: carried over from 20-18, not new.** It is at 20-18 ¶10.B and 20-19 ¶10.B,
  same text.
- **¶5.A(2) Legal Holiday extension:** present in 20-18, carried over (wording tightened only).
- **¶2, ¶4, ¶9, ¶11, ¶13-¶16, ¶18, ¶19, ¶23:** no substantive change.

## Paragraph-by-paragraph reference

### ¶1 PARTIES (page 1)
Keys: `seller_names`, `buyer_names`. Legal names, not nicknames. Include the marital recital where
community property applies (Tex. Fam. Code §3.002). Must match the execution block exactly.

### ¶2 PROPERTY (page 1) — unchanged
- **2.A LAND:** Lot / Block / Addition / City of / County of / Texas, known as (address/zip), "or as
  described on attached exhibit."
- **2.B IMPROVEMENTS:** fixtures list, includes generators.
- **2.C ACCESSORIES:** window A/C, stove, keys, above-ground pool, security systems that are not
  fixtures, and **Controls** for garage doors / entry gates / other — "Controls" expressly includes
  Seller's transferable rights to the software and applications used to access and control them.
- **2.D EXCLUSIONS:** free-text, Seller-retained, must be removed prior to delivery of possession.
- **2.E RESERVATIONS:** any reservation of oil, gas, other minerals, water, timber, or other
  interests is made "in accordance with an attached addendum."

### ¶3 SALES PRICE (page 1) — UNCHANGED (see correction note)
3.A cash portion · 3.B sum of all financing, with **three** addendum checkboxes · 3.C total.
**A + B must equal C.** 3.A carries the caution that "Cash portion" excludes borrowing proceeds or
proceeds of selling other real property except as disclosed.

### ¶4 LEASES (page 1) — unchanged
Intro: after the Effective Date Seller may not, without Buyer's written consent, create a new lease,
amend an existing lease, or convey any interest. **Check all applicable boxes** (not one-of).
- 4.A residential leases → Addendum Regarding Residential Leases.
- 4.B fixture leases (solar panels, propane tanks, water softener, security system) → Addendum
  Regarding Fixture Leases.
- 4.C natural resource leases, with paired (1) delivered / (2) not delivered; (2) obliges Seller to
  provide copies within 3 days after Effective Date and gives Buyer ____ days after receipt to
  terminate with earnest money refunded.

*Locked convention:* the paired 4.C boxes share key `natural_resource_leases_delivered`; the engine
inverts. Never split into a `_not_delivered` key.

### ¶5 EARNEST MONEY AND TERMINATION OPTION (page 2) — unchanged in substance
- **5.A** Both earnest money **and** option fee delivered to Escrow Agent within **3 days after the
  Effective Date**; payable to Escrow Agent; may be paid separately or combined.
- **5.A(1)** additional earnest money $____ within ____ days after Effective Date.
- **5.A(2)** Legal Holiday extension — if the last day falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or Legal Holiday
  (as defined by Tex. Gov't Code §§662.003(a), (b)(4), (6)), extend to the next day that is not one.
- **5.A(3)** Application order: **option fee first, then earnest money, then additional earnest money.**
- **5.A(4)** Escrow Agent may release the option fee to Seller at any time without further notice;
  the option fee **is credited to the Sales Price at closing**.
- **5.B TERMINATION OPTION:** unrestricted right to terminate by notice within ____ days after the
  Effective Date (Option Period). **Notice must be given by 5:00 p.m. local time where the Property
  is located** on the date specified. On timely termination: option fee not refunded, earnest money
  refunded.
- **5.C** Buyer fails to timely deliver earnest money → Seller may terminate or invoke ¶15, or both,
  by notice **before Buyer delivers**.
- **5.D** No dollar amount stated as the option fee, **or** failure to timely deliver it → **no
  unrestricted termination right**. (A blank option fee kills the option. Check it every time.)
- **5.E** Time is of the essence for ¶5; strict compliance required.

### ¶6 TITLE POLICY AND SURVEY (pages 2-4)
- **6.A** Title Policy at ☐Seller's ☐Buyer's expense, issued by (Title Company), amount = Sales
  Price, subject to exceptions **(1)-(9)**. Sub-item (8) has its own ☐(i) not amended / ☐(ii)
  amended to "shortages in area" at the expense of ☐Buyer ☐Seller.
- **6.B COMMITMENT:** within **20 days** after the Title Company receives a copy of the contract;
  automatic extension up to **15 days** or 3 days before Closing Date, whichever is earlier; failure
  → Buyer may terminate, earnest money refunded.
- **6.C SURVEY: check one box only** — (1) Seller furnishes existing survey + T-47 Affidavit or
  T-47.1 Declaration within ____ days; (2) Buyer may obtain new survey at Buyer's expense within
  ____ days; (3) Seller furnishes a new survey at Seller's expense within ____ days.
  Note (2)'s trap: if Buyer fails to obtain the survey, Buyer **may not** terminate under ¶2B of the
  Third Party Financing Addendum for lack of a survey.
- **6.D OBJECTIONS:** Buyer objects to matters on the survey other than 6.A(1)-(7), or in the
  Commitment other than 6.A(1)-(9), or which prohibit a stated use/activity. Deadline: **earlier of
  the Closing Date or ____ days after** receipt of Commitment, Exception Documents, and survey.
  **Cure Period = 15 days.** Buyer's post-cure decision: **5 days** after end of Cure Period to
  terminate or waive; silence = waiver. Schedule C requirements are not waived.
- **6.E TITLE NOTICES: (1) through (11).** (1) abstract/title policy advice · (2) POA mandatory
  membership, with ☐is ☐is not · (3) statutory tax districts · (4) tide waters · (5) annexation /
  ETJ · (6) certificated service area of a utility service provider · (7) public improvement
  districts · (8) transfer fees · (9) propane gas system service area · (10) water level
  fluctuations · (11) certificate of mold remediation (5 years preceding the sale).
  **Ends at (11). There is no (12) and no (13).**

### ¶7 PROPERTY CONDITION (pages 4-5)
- **7.A** Access, inspections, utilities. Inspectors licensed by TREC or otherwise permitted by law.
  **Hydrostatic testing must be separately authorized by Seller in writing.** Seller keeps utilities
  on, at Seller's expense, for the life of the contract.
- **7.B SELLER'S DISCLOSURE NOTICE** per Tex. Prop. Code §5.008 — **check one box only**:
  (1) received · (2) not received, Seller delivers within ____ days; if never received Buyer may
  terminate **any time prior to closing**; if delivered late, Buyer may terminate for any reason
  **within 7 days after receipt** or prior to closing, whichever first occurs · (3) Seller not
  required to furnish it.
- **7.C** Seller's disclosure of lead-based paint and LBP hazards "is required by Federal law for a
  residential dwelling constructed prior to 1978." Statement only — **no checkbox, no form number**
  in ¶7.C itself; the addendum checkbox lives in ¶22.
- **7.D ACCEPTANCE OF PROPERTY CONDITION** — "As Is" defined; accepting As Is does **not** preclude
  inspecting under 7.A, negotiating repairs in a later amendment, or terminating during the Option
  Period. **Check one box only:** (1) As Is · (2) As Is provided Seller completes specific repairs
  and treatments at Seller's expense (+ blank). Form warns: do not insert general phrases such as
  "subject to inspections."
- **7.E LENDER REQUIRED REPAIRS:** neither party obligated to pay; includes wood-destroying-insect
  treatment; no agreement → contract terminates, earnest money refunded. If cost **exceeds 5% of the
  Sales Price**, Buyer may terminate, earnest money refunded.
- **7.F COMPLETION OF REPAIRS:** before Closing Date, permits obtained, performed by licensed or
  commercially-engaged persons; Seller provides scope-of-work and payment documentation and
  transfers transferable warranties at Seller's expense. Seller's failure → Buyer may invoke ¶15 or
  **extend the Closing Date up to 5 days**.
- **7.G ENVIRONMENTAL MATTERS:** wetlands, toxic substances, asbestos, wastes, threatened/endangered
  species or habitat.
- **7.H RESIDENTIAL SERVICE CONTRACTS:** provider/administrator licensed by TDLR; Seller reimburses
  Buyer at closing up to $______. Purchase is optional.
- **7.I SELLER'S WATER DISCLOSURE — NEW.** See Change 1 above for the full breakdown and the
  post-option termination right.

### ¶8 BROKER OR SALES AGENT DISCLOSURE (pages 5-6) — RENAMED, 8.B DELETED
Single block, free-text "Disclose if applicable" lines. See Change 3.

### ¶9 CLOSING (page 6) — unchanged
- **9.A** on or before ______, 20___, **or within 7 days after ¶6.D objections are cured or waived,
  whichever is later**. Failure to close → non-defaulting party may invoke ¶15.
- **9.B** at-closing duties **(1)-(5)**: general warranty deed + tax statements; Buyer pays in good
  funds; execution of closing documents; no unsatisfied liens except assumed loans; private transfer
  fees are Seller's obligation unless otherwise provided.

### ¶10 POSSESSION (page 6) — unchanged
- **10.A** ☐upon closing and funding ☐according to a temporary residential lease. Unauthorized
  possession creates a **tenancy at sufferance**. Insurance warning is in the form.
- **10.B SMART DEVICES** — **carried over from 20-18, NOT new.** At delivery of possession Seller
  must (1) deliver written access codes, usernames, passwords, and applications, and (2) terminate
  and remove all access and connections from Seller's personal devices.

### ¶11 SPECIAL PROVISIONS (page 6) — unchanged
Informational items only. Brokers and sales agents are prohibited from practicing law and "shall not
add to, delete, or modify any provision of this contract" unless drafted by a party or a party's
attorney (TRELA §1101.654 territory). Factual statements only.

### ¶12 SETTLEMENT AND OTHER EXPENSES (pages 6-7) — RESTRUCTURED
- **12.A(1)** Seller's Expenses — (a) releases of liens, prepayment penalties, recording fees,
  release of Seller's loan liability, tax statements/certificates, preparation of deed, one-half of
  escrow fee (**"brokerage fees that Seller has agreed to pay" struck**); (b) amount not to exceed
  $______ applied to Buyer's Expenses **other than brokerage compensation or ¶12.B contributions**.
- **12.A(2)** Buyer's Expenses — the long list (**"brokerage fees that Buyer has agreed to pay"
  struck**).
- **12.B BROKERAGE COMPENSATION — NEW, two-way.** See Change 2.
- **12.C EXPENSE LIMITATION — NEW and narrower.** The old excess-expense termination right is gone.

### ¶13 PRORATIONS (page 7) — unchanged
Taxes, interest, rents, regular periodic maintenance fees, assessments, dues prorated through the
Closing Date; proration may account for a change in exemptions; parties adjust when actual statements
issue. *(Relevant to Heath's practice: a homestead/ag exemption lost on transfer swings this
materially — see the 23 Nopalito file.)*

### ¶14 CASUALTY LOSS (page 7) — unchanged
Seller restores by the Closing Date; if Seller can't due to factors beyond Seller's control, Buyer
may (a) terminate with earnest money refunded, (b) extend up to 15 days, or (c) accept as-damaged
with assignment of insurance proceeds plus credit for the deductible.

### ¶15 DEFAULT (page 7) — unchanged
Buyer defaults → Seller may enforce specific performance / other relief, or terminate and receive the
earnest money as liquidated damages. Seller defaults → Buyer may enforce specific performance / other
relief, or terminate and receive the earnest money.

### ¶16 MEDIATION (page 7) — unchanged
Disputes not resolved informally go to a mutually acceptable mediator; costs borne equally; does not
preclude equitable relief from a court.

### ¶17 ATTORNEY'S FEES (page 7) — broker terms renamed
"A Buyer, Seller, **Seller's broker, Buyer's broker**, or Escrow Agent who prevails ... is entitled
to recover reasonable attorney's fees and all costs."

## Common Q&A

**Q1. 20-18 or 20-19?**
20-19 for any offer drafted on or after 2026-07-01. Contracts already executed on 20-18 stay on
20-18 — you don't re-paper mid-transaction. Using a superseded promulgated form on a new offer is a
TRELA §1101.652(b)(22) discipline risk.

**Q2. What actually changed?**
Six things: new ¶7.I Water Disclosure; brokerage compensation out of ¶12.A(1)(b) into a new two-way
¶12.B (and old ¶12.B's excess-expense termination right deleted, replaced by a narrower ¶12.C); ¶8
renamed with the brokers'-fees clause deleted; ¶6.E(12) deleted and moved into ¶22; ¶22 regrouped
into five categories; ¶20 renamed with a new 20.B, fax dropped from ¶21, and the page-11 broker
compensation disclosure deleted. The form went from 11 pages to 12.

**Q3. Is the ¶3.B financing checkbox layout different?**
No. Three boxes in 20-18, three boxes in 20-19, identical text. (An earlier version of this file said
otherwise. It was wrong.)

**Q4. Is Smart Devices new?**
No. ¶10.B in both forms. If you're pitching it as a 20-19 change on camera or in content, you'll be
corrected by the first experienced agent who sees it.

**Q5. Do I need the Water Disclosure on every contract?**
No — but you must make an **election at ¶7.I on every contract**, and that's different from "attach
it every time." On a typical platted city lot on municipal water, 7.I(3) applies and you name the
provider. On anything rural, with a well, a pond/lake/tank, TCEQ surface-water rights, or severed
groundwater rights, 7.I(3) is unavailable and you're in (1) or (2). Getting this wrong on the seller
side leaves Buyer a termination right that lasts until closing.

**Q6. My buyer's option period expired. Is the water disclosure issue dead?**
**No — and this is the one to remember.** ¶7.I(2)'s remedy is not tied to the Option Period. If the
Seller never delivered the Water Disclosure, Buyer may terminate **at any time prior to closing** with
earnest money refunded. Same structure as ¶7.B for the Seller's Disclosure Notice.

**Q7. Where do I put a seller contribution to my buyer's broker now?**
¶12.B(1). Not ¶12.A(1)(b) — that blank is now expressly "other than brokerage compensation." Check the
12.B(1) enabling box, then the $ or % sub-box, then fill the blank.

**Q8. Does a ¶12.B seller contribution change what my client owes me?**
No, and the form says so in bold. The contribution is applied to, and does not change, the
compensation obligation in your separate written agreement. If your buyer-rep says 2.5% and the seller
contributes 2%, your client owes the remainder. Say it out loud at the table.

**Q9. 1985 build — lead paint?**
No. The federal requirement (24 CFR §35) is for dwellings **built before 1978**. Verify the year built
from CAD before relying on the exemption.

**Q10. Which paragraph has the Effective Date?**
None — it's the **unnumbered execution block on page 10**. The broker fills in the date of final
acceptance. Every "days after the Effective Date" clock runs from it.

**Q11. Can I still fax a notice?**
Not under ¶21 — fax was removed as a delivery method. Mail, hand delivery, overnight courier, or
electronic transmission. (¶23 attorney blocks and the page-12 receipts still have fax *fields*; that's
contact information, not a ¶21 delivery method.)

## Common practitioner mistakes

1. **Drafting a new offer on 20-18 after 2026-07-01.** Discipline risk under TRELA §1101.652(b)(22).
2. **Leaving ¶7.I unelected.** It's check-one-box-only and it's easy to scroll past at the bottom of
   page 5. An unelected 7.I on the seller side is an open-ended buyer exit.
3. **Checking ¶7.I(3) without testing all five conditions** — especially a not-in-use well, or
   failing to name the water provider in (e).
4. **Putting the seller's buyer-broker contribution in ¶12.A(1)(b).** Wrong blank now.
5. **Hunting for ¶6.E(12).** It's gone; the district notices go on the ¶22 Statutory Disclosures line.
6. **Finding a ¶22 addendum box by position.** The list is regrouped — read the group headers.
7. **Assuming the old ¶12.B excess-expense termination right still exists.** It was deleted outright.
8. **Blank option fee amount in ¶5.A.** Per ¶5.D, no stated dollar amount = no unrestricted
   termination right, even if the buyer thinks they bought an option period.
9. **Porting a 20-18 field map to 20-19 for ¶21 or page 11.** Both were restructured. Use the coords
   asset.
10. Carried from 20-18: nicknames instead of legal names, missing marital recital, MLS-only legal
    description, unchecked ¶3.B, forgetting a solar lease under ¶4.B, missing the 5:00 p.m. option
    deadline, blank Effective Date.
