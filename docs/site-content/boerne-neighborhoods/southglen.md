# Southglen, Boerne, Texas

Southglen sits on the south side of Boerne near Cascade Caverns Road, and its streets are relentlessly Scottish — Abbeydell, Aberdeen, Ayshire, Bellgrove, Braeburn, Breamoor, Clarendon, Edinburgh, Esslemont, Kilmarnock, Wickersham. It is a substantial, established subdivision that has recently seen new construction added to older phases, which makes it two neighborhoods in one.

## The county question — and why Southglen is the exception

Most homes with a **Boerne, TX 78015** mailing address are in **Bexar County**. Balcones Creek, Front Gate, Napa Oaks, Fallbrook, Sablechase, Anaqua Springs Ranch and Lost Creek are all Bexar County properties with Boerne mail.

**Southglen is not.** It is in **Kendall County**, and it is inside the **City of Boerne** city limits.

Kendall County Appraisal District parcel records show the County of Kendall and the **City of Boerne** as taxing units on all 432 Southglen parcels (Kendall CAD GIS parcel layer, retrieved September 2026). Independently, the association's amended management certificate recorded in March 2026 cites every foundational document — the original declaration, multiple declarations of annexation, deeds without warranty, and even the swimming pool policies manual — as recorded in the **Official Public Records of Kendall County, Texas**.

Two independent sources, same answer. If you see Southglen described as a Bexar County neighborhood, that is wrong, and the difference shows up on the tax bill.

## The numbers

Kendall CAD parcel records show **432 parcels across roughly 117 acres**, with **391 already improved** (retrieved September 2026). Roughly 90% built out.

Lots: 10th percentile about **0.122 acres**, median **0.153 acres**, 90th percentile about **0.279 acres**. Small suburban lots.

Current Kendall CAD market values on improved parcels: **median near $432,000**, 10th percentile around $362,000, 90th around $523,000. That is one of the more affordable established neighborhoods inside Boerne city limits. Appraisal-district values, not asking prices.

## Housing stock

Two distinct vintages. The original phases date back to an earlier era of the subdivision — the declaration carries an older-style Kendall County document number consistent with a 1990s filing — while a current phase is being built new by **Ashton Woods**, with plans running roughly 1,637 to 3,104 square feet in three to five bedroom configurations.

That split matters. A resale in an original phase and a new Ashton Woods home in the current phase are different products at different price points inside the same HOA. Know which one you are touring.

## Jurisdiction and utilities

Inside the **City of Boerne**, per Kendall CAD. That means City of Boerne water and sewer service and city trash, with Bandera Electric Cooperative serving power in this area. City police. A city tax line on the bill.

For a neighborhood on eighth-to-sixth-acre lots, city utilities are the right answer, and it is a meaningful advantage over comparably priced inventory further out.

## HOA

**The HOA is mandatory.** The association is **Southglen Residential Community, Inc.**, a Texas nonprofit corporation. Note that the association's registered mailing address is in San Antonio 78258 — that is the management company's office, not the neighborhood's location. Do not read the management address as evidence of where the subdivision is; that mistake is exactly how Southglen gets mislabeled as a Bexar County community.

**The recurring dues amount is not disclosed** on the management certificate. A single resale listing cited $113 per quarter covering pool access, but that is one data point, not a confirmed fee schedule. Confirm the current assessment with the association before your option period ends.

## Amenities

Southglen has a **community pool** — the association's own filed certificate references a recorded Swimming Pool Policies and Procedures Manual, which is about as solid a confirmation as you get. Ashton Woods also markets a cabana, playscape, and hiking trails with the current phase.

Verify which amenities are available to which phases. In subdivisions that add new sections under a shared association, amenity access is occasionally phased too.

We found no source confirming the neighborhood is gated.

## Schools

**Boerne ISD.** A point inside the neighborhood returns **Kendall Elementary, Boerne Middle School South, and Champion High School** through Boerne ISD's own InfoFinder lookup (queried September 2026).

Confirm your specific address. Boerne ISD opened Viola Wilson Elementary in August 2024 and redrew elementary boundaries then — and Southglen sits in the part of the district where that redraw was most active. Nearby Balcones Creek and Kendall Creek Estates both now feed Viola Wilson. A Southglen listing that names an elementary school without a date on the claim should not be trusted.

## Location and commute

South Boerne off Old Fredericksburg Road and Cascade Caverns Road, east of I-10. The interstate access is good, which makes the San Antonio commute among the more manageable ones from a Boerne address. Downtown Boerne and the Highway 46 retail corridor are short drives.

## Who it suits

It suits a buyer who wants an established Boerne address inside the city limits, a community pool, city utilities, and a price point below most of the newer Kendall County inventory. It suits commuters. It suits people who want to choose between a resale and a brand-new build without leaving the neighborhood.

It does not suit a buyer who wants land, trees, or privacy. It does not suit someone who wants uniformity — the old and new phases do not look alike. And it does not suit anyone underwriting a payment on the assumption that a 78015 address means Bexar County tax rates.
