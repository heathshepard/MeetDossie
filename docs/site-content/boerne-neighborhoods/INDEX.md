# Boerne & Kendall County Neighborhood Guides — Index

39 neighborhood guide pages covering Boerne, Texas (78006 and 78015) and the surrounding Kendall County area, plus the Bexar County communities that carry a Boerne mailing address.

**Last verified:** September 2026.

---

## How the facts on these pages were established

Every page was built from primary or government sources first, with real estate marketing pages used only where nothing better existed and labeled as such.

| Fact type | Source used | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| County, city/jurisdiction, school district (Kendall) | Kendall County Appraisal District parcel GIS — `county`, `city`, `school` taxing-unit fields on every parcel | Authoritative |
| Parcel counts, acreage, market values (Kendall) | Kendall CAD parcel GIS, current appraisal roll | Authoritative |
| County, parcel counts, year built, values (Bexar) | Bexar County parcel GIS (`maps.bexar.org`) | Authoritative |
| School district (Bexar) | Bexar County School Districts GIS layer | Authoritative |
| Attendance zones (campus level) | Boerne ISD's own InfoFinder address/point lookup, queried by parcel centroid | Authoritative |
| HOA existence, legal name, manager, transfer fees | TREC/Texas HOA management certificates filed under Tex. Prop. Code § 209.004, via `hoa.texas.gov` | Authoritative (filed legal instrument) |
| Historic district rules | City of Boerne | Authoritative |
| Recurring HOA dues, amenities, developer names, build history | Association websites, builder pages, brokerage marketing | Weak — flagged inline |

**The single most important limitation:** Texas management certificates are only required to disclose **transfer-related fees** (resale certificate, transfer fee, working capital). They do **not** disclose recurring dues. As a result, **recurring HOA dues could not be verified from a filed document for any community in this set.** Every page says so and directs the reader to the association. Do not let a dues figure be added to any page without a primary source.

## The three highest-value corrections in this set

These are facts that are commonly published wrong across Boerne-area real estate sites, and that a buyer could act on to their cost.

1. **Anaqua Springs Ranch, Sundance Ranch and Lost Creek are in NORTHSIDE ISD, not Boerne ISD.** All three carry Boerne mailing addresses; two carry the 78006 zip. Verified two independent ways each (Bexar County school district GIS, plus nine-point probes of Boerne ISD's own InfoFinder returning no assignment).
2. **A Boerne mailing address does not mean Kendall County.** Nine communities in this set are legally in Bexar County, including Balcones Creek and Highlands Ranch, which use Boerne's own **78006** zip. Conversely, **Southglen and Kendall Pointe carry 78015 addresses but are in Kendall County.** The zip code is a postal route, not a taxing jurisdiction.
3. **Boerne ISD opened Viola Wilson Elementary in August 2024 and redrew elementary boundaries**, moving roughly 469 students. Any elementary-school claim published before that date is suspect. Three neighborhoods in this set (Balcones Creek, Highlands Ranch, Kendall Creek Estates) now feed Wilson.

Secondary corrections worth keeping: **Champion Heights is zoned to Boerne High School, not Champion.** **The Woods of Boerne sits across the highway from Champion High School and is zoned to Boerne High School.** **Willis Ranch was investigated and dropped** — it is at zip 78260 near Bulverde, not a Boerne-area community.
