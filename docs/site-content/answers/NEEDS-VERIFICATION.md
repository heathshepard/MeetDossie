## TIER 1 — close these before the pages go live

### 1. FHA loan limit for Kendall County / San Antonio–New Braunfels MSA, CY2026
**Wanted:** the exact one-unit FHA forward mortgage limit for Kendall County in 2026.
**Verified:** the nationwide **floor is $541,287** and the **ceiling is $1,249,125**, per HUD
Mortgagee Letter 2025-23, effective for case numbers assigned January 1 – December 31, 2026.
<https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/hudclips/documents/2025-23hsgml.pdf>
**Not verified:** a secondary source reports the San Antonio–New Braunfels MSA one-unit limit at
**$557,750** (2-unit $714,000, 3-unit $863,100, 4-unit $1,072,600). The page attributes it and
routes readers to HUD rather than stating it.
**How to close it:** HUD's FHA Mortgage Limits lookup,
<https://entp.hud.gov/idapp/html/hicostlook.cfm> — State TX, County Kendall, Limit Type FHA
Forward, Limit Year CY2026. The lookup is a ColdFusion form that rejected scripted POSTs in this
pass and needs a real browser session. Alternative: HUD's downloadable county-level CY2026 loan
limit file.

### 2. Circuit breaker (Tax Code §23.231) value threshold
**The conflict:** Travis Central Appraisal District states **"$5.16 million or less"**
(<https://traviscad.org/circuitbreaker>); a secondary tax-consulting source states **$5,320,000
for tax year 2026**. The threshold is indexed annually, so the two figures may belong to different
tax years, but that was not established.
**What the pages publish:** the 20 percent cap, applicability to tax years 2024, 2025 and 2026,
expiration after the 2026 tax year absent legislative renewal, that no application is required
because the district applies it automatically, and that the owner must have held the property at
least one full calendar year. **No dollar threshold.**
**How to close it:** Texas Comptroller property tax publications, or Kendall Appraisal District —
<https://kendallad.org/>.

### 3. Whether the circuit breaker is renewed after the 2026 tax year
The pilot is authorized for tax years 2024–2026 and expires after the 2026 tax year unless the
Legislature renews it. As of September 2026 no renewal has been verified. **Three pages state that
expiration date.** If the Legislature renews it, those pages go stale immediately.
**Set a review trigger for the next regular session.**

### 4. Whether Kendall County carries the baseline conforming loan limit
The **2026 baseline conforming one-unit limit is $832,750**, up $26,250 from 2025's $806,500, with
a high-cost ceiling of $1,249,125 (FHFA,
<https://www.fhfa.gov/news/news-release/fhfa-announces-conforming-loan-limit-values-for-2026>).
Whether Kendall County is a baseline county or carries a high-cost designation was **not
confirmed against FHFA's county-level table**. The page is written around it and tells the reader
the lender confirms it. Close against FHFA's 2026 county loan limit file.

## TIER 2 — wanted, omitted, low risk

### 5. HOA resale certificate *update* fee cap
The **$375 cap** on assembling, copying and delivering subdivision information is verified directly
from **Texas Property Code §207.003(c)** and is published as fact
(<https://codes.findlaw.com/tx/property-code/prop-sect-207-003/>), as is the **10-business-day**
delivery deadline in §207.003(a). The widely repeated **$75 cap on an updated certificate** could
not be located in the statute text retrieved. It is omitted from every page.

### 6. 1031 exchange identification rules beyond the 45-day deadline
The **45-day identification** requirement and the **180-day / tax-return-due-date, whichever is
earlier** rule are verified from IRS material. The commonly taught **three-property rule** and
**200 percent rule** were not verified and are omitted. Close against Treas. Reg. §1.1031(k)-1.

### 7. Texas tax statements issuing late in the calendar year
The proration page explains why the current year's tax figure is usually not final at closing. That
framing is written as how the calendar works, **not as a statutory citation**. The page's
substance rests on **¶13 of TREC 20-19**, which is quoted in full and verified. Close the arrears
framing against Tax Code ch. 31 or a county tax assessor-collector page if a citation is wanted.

### 8. Over-65 ceiling details not stated
Three specifics were deliberately left off the over-65 page because they could not be sourced: the
**surviving-spouse age threshold** for continuing the school tax ceiling (Tax Code §11.26), how the
first year of eligibility is handled when an owner **turns 65 mid-year**, and the **length of the
payment window** after a deferral ends (Tax Code §33.06 subsections). The verified parts — the
ceiling itself, the percentage transfer, Form 50-311, Form 50-126 and the five percent deferral
interest under §33.06(d) — are published.

## STANDING RULES THAT PRODUCED THIS LIST

1. **No lending number is published without a primary source.** No minimum credit scores, no
   minimum down payment percentages, no VA funding fee percentages, no FHA MIP or PMI rates, no
   DTI ratios, no interest rates appear anywhere in the library. The financing pages explain
   program structure versus lender overlay and route to a lender. That is a deliberate editorial
   choice, not a gap.
2. **No closing-cost percentage of sales price is published.** The pages enumerate the actual
   ¶12A(1) and ¶12A(2) line items from the contract and route to the lender's Loan Estimate and
   the title company's fee sheet.
3. **No title insurance premium dollar figure is published.** Texas premium rates are promulgated
   by the Texas Department of Insurance, so the pages link the official rate table
   (<https://tdi.texas.gov/title/titlerates2026.html>, effective March 1, 2026) rather than quote a
   number that would go stale.
4. **No occupancy rates, ADRs, RevPAR, cap rates, rents, land prices or appreciation rates** appear
   on any investing page.
5. **Nothing about the agent's production is stated anywhere** — no closing counts, no years in
   business, no awards, no testimonials, no client anecdotes. Those are the site owner's to add
   from his own records.
6. **Boerne is Kendall County.** San Antonio ordinances do not govern it and the appraisal district
   is Kendall AD, not Bexar CAD. Every page where a reader could assume otherwise says so.
