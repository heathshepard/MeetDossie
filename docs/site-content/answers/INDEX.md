It is not the City of San Antonio and not Bexar County**, and every page in this group says so where it matters.

### [Does Boerne, Texas flood?](./does-boerne-flood.md)

Whether a particular property floods is determined by its FEMA flood zone, the seller's disclosure under Texas Property Code 5.008, and an insurance quote. A property outside the mapped Special Flood Hazard Area can still flood; the map is not a risk assessment.

## Texas transaction mechanics

How a Texas residential deal actually runs, cited to the current promulgated TREC forms and, where it matters, to the paragraph. The resale contract is **TREC form 20-19, effective 07/01/2026**.

### [Do I need a real estate attorney to buy a house in Texas?](./do-i-need-a-real-estate-attorney-to-buy-a-house-in-texas.md)

`/answers/do-i-need-a-real-estate-attorney-to-buy-a-house-in-texas`

> No. Texas does not require a real estate attorney to buy a house. The title company handles escrow, title search and closing documents, and license holders use TREC promulgated forms. An attorney is the right call for title defects, probate or estate sales, deed drafting, deed restriction questions, and any custom contract language.

### [How do I terminate a real estate contract in Texas?](./how-do-i-terminate-a-contract-in-texas.md)

`/answers/how-do-i-terminate-a-contract-in-texas`

> You terminate a Texas contract by exercising a right the contract gives you and delivering written notice — most commonly during the option period using TREC form 38-8. Terminating the contract and getting the earnest money back are two separate events: escrowed funds cannot be released without mutual agreement or a court order.

### [How does the option fee work in Texas?](./how-does-the-option-fee-work-in-texas.md)

`/answers/how-does-the-option-fee-work-in-texas`

> The option fee is a negotiated amount the buyer delivers to the escrow agent — the title company, not the seller — within three days after the effective date. It pays for the unrestricted right to terminate. If the sale closes, the fee is credited to the sales price. If the buyer terminates, the seller keeps it.

### [Is the option fee refundable in Texas?](./is-the-option-fee-refundable-in-texas.md)

`/answers/is-the-option-fee-refundable-in-texas`

> No. The option fee is not refundable. If the buyer terminates during the option period, the seller keeps it — that is what the fee bought. If the sale closes, the fee is credited to the sales price, so the buyer effectively gets it back at the closing table. Earnest money is the refundable deposit.

### [What does the effective date mean in a Texas real estate contract?](./what-does-the-effective-date-mean-in-a-texas-contract.md)

`/answers/what-does-the-effective-date-mean-in-a-texas-contract`

> The effective date is the date the last party signs the contract and that signature is delivered. It is day zero. Every other deadline in a Texas contract counts from it: the option fee delivery, the option period, the financing deadline, title objections and the closing date. A wrong effective date makes every one of them wrong.

### [What happens at a Texas real estate closing?](./what-happens-at-a-texas-closing.md)

`/answers/what-happens-at-a-texas-closing`

> At a Texas closing the title company acts as escrow agent. The parties sign the deed, note and closing disclosure, the buyer wires funds, the lender funds the loan, and the title company records the deed. Signing is not closing — the deal closes when it funds and records. No attorney is required.

### [What is a MUD or a PID in a Texas subdivision?](./what-is-a-mud-or-pid-in-a-texas-subdivision.md)

`/answers/what-is-a-mud-or-pid-in-a-texas-subdivision`

> A MUD is a municipal utility district and a PID is a public improvement district. Both fund water, sewer, roads and amenities in a Texas subdivision, and both add a line to the property tax bill that can change the monthly payment. Each has a TREC notice form that goes to the buyer in the transaction.

### [What is a T-47 affidavit in Texas?](./what-is-a-t-47-affidavit.md)

`/answers/what-is-a-t-47-affidavit`

> A T-47 is a notarized affidavit in which a Texas seller states that nothing has changed on the property since the existing survey. With it, the lender and title company can often accept that survey instead of requiring a new one. A T-47.1 declaration does the same job without notarization; both took effect November 1, 2024.

### [What is a title commitment in Texas?](./what-is-a-title-commitment-in-texas.md)

`/answers/what-is-a-title-commitment-in-texas`

> A title commitment is the title company's written promise to issue a title policy on stated terms. It comes in four schedules: A is the deal facts, B is what the policy will not cover, C is what must be fixed before closing, and D discloses who gets paid. Reading it is a task with a deadline.

### [What is an HOA resale certificate in Texas?](./what-is-an-hoa-resale-certificate-in-texas.md)

`/answers/what-is-an-hoa-resale-certificate-in-texas`

> An HOA resale certificate is a Texas Property Code chapter 207 document the property owners' association prepares for a pending sale. It states assessments, unpaid amounts, known violations, insurance, pending lawsuits, reserves and transfer restrictions. The association must deliver it within 10 business days of a proper written request, prepared no earlier than 60 days before delivery.

### [What is earnest money in Texas, and is it refundable?](./what-is-earnest-money-in-texas-and-is-it-refundable.md)

`/answers/what-is-earnest-money-in-texas-and-is-it-refundable`

> Earnest money is a good-faith deposit the buyer delivers to the title company to be held in escrow. It is refundable if the buyer terminates during the option period or under a contract right such as the financing addendum. It is credited to the buyer at closing and cannot be released without mutual agreement or a court order.

### [What is the Amendment to Contract in a Texas real estate deal?](./what-is-the-amendment-to-contract-form-in-texas.md)

`/answers/what-is-the-amendment-to-contract-form-in-texas`

> The Amendment to Contract is the TREC form used to change the terms of a contract that is already in effect: price, closing date, repairs, option period. It takes effect only when every party signs and it is delivered. The current version is TREC form 39-11, effective 07/01/2026. It changes a contract. It does not end one.

### [What is the Seller's Disclosure Notice in Texas?](./what-is-the-sellers-disclosure-notice-in-texas.md)

`/answers/what-is-the-sellers-disclosure-notice-in-texas`

> The Seller's Disclosure Notice is a written notice Texas Property Code section 5.008 requires from a seller of residential property with not more than one dwelling unit. It discloses known condition, defects and flood history. If the seller delivers it after the contract is signed, the buyer may terminate for any reason within seven days after receiving it.

### [What is the TREC One to Four Family Residential Contract?](./what-is-the-trec-1-4-family-residential-contract.md)

`/answers/what-is-the-trec-1-4-family-residential-contract`

> The One to Four Family Residential Contract (Resale) is the TREC promulgated form Texas license holders use to sell existing single-family homes, duplexes, triplexes and four-plexes. The current version is form 20-19, effective July 1, 2026. Condominiums, builder new construction and farm and ranch each use a different form.

### [What is the Third Party Financing Addendum in Texas?](./what-is-the-third-party-financing-addendum.md)

`/answers/what-is-the-third-party-financing-addendum`

> The Third Party Financing Addendum is the TREC form that makes a Texas contract conditional on the buyer's loan. It identifies the loan type and terms, and it gives the buyer a deadline to terminate if the lender will not approve the buyer. The current version is TREC form 40-11, effective 01/03/2025.

### [What is the option period in a Texas real estate contract?](./what-is-the-option-period-in-texas.md)

`/answers/what-is-the-option-period-in-texas`

> The option period is a negotiated window in the TREC contract during which a Texas buyer can terminate for any reason and get their earnest money back. The buyer pays a separate option fee to the title company within three days of the effective date. The fee is credited at closing and is not refunded on termination.

### [What is title insurance in Texas, and why is the price the same everywhere?](./what-is-title-insurance-in-texas.md)

`/answers/what-is-title-insurance-in-texas`

> Texas title insurance premium rates are promulgated by the Texas Department of Insurance, so the premium for a given policy amount is identical at every title company in Texas. Shopping title companies is about service, escrow fees and closing experience, not premium price. The current rate schedule took effect March 1, 2026.

### [Who pays closing costs in Texas?](./who-pays-closing-costs-in-texas.md)

`/answers/who-pays-closing-costs-in-texas`

> Who pays closing costs in Texas is negotiated in the contract, not fixed by law. Custom in much of Texas is that the seller pays the owner's title policy and the buyer pays lender and loan-related costs, but every line is negotiable. Your lender's Loan Estimate and the title company's fee sheet give the actual numbers.

### [Who pays for the survey in a Texas real estate transaction?](./who-pays-for-the-survey-in-texas.md)

`/answers/who-pays-for-the-survey-in-texas`

> Who pays for the survey is negotiated in the contract, not set by Texas law. The TREC contract has a paragraph where the parties choose. Custom in much of Texas is that the seller furnishes an existing survey with a T-47 affidavit, and the buyer pays for a new one if the existing survey is not acceptable.

## Taxes and exemptions

Texas property tax as a homeowner meets it: the 2026 exemption amounts, the caps, the protest, and the exemptions that carry real money. Every valuation or exemption decision routes to the appraisal district — **Kendall Appraisal District** for a Boerne property — and every tax consequence routes to a CPA.

### [Are property taxes prorated at closing in Texas?](./are-property-taxes-prorated-at-closing-in-texas.md)

`/answers/are-property-taxes-prorated-at-closing-in-texas`

> Yes. Under Paragraph 13 of the TREC 20-19 contract, taxes for the current year are prorated through the Closing Date. The title company allocates the year between seller and buyer on the settlement statement, the proration may take a change in exemptions into account, and the parties must adjust it once the actual tax statements are available.

### [Can I defer my property taxes in Texas if I'm over 65?](./can-i-defer-my-property-taxes-in-texas-over-65.md)

`/answers/can-i-defer-my-property-taxes-in-texas-over-65`

> Yes. A Texas homeowner 65 or older can defer property taxes on their residence homestead by filing Comptroller Form 50-126 with the appraisal district. Deferral stops foreclosure and delinquency penalties while it is in effect, but the taxes keep accruing at 5 percent annual interest under Tax Code 33.06 and eventually come due.

### [How do I file for a homestead exemption in Kendall County, Texas?](./how-do-i-file-for-a-homestead-exemption-in-kendall-county.md)

`/answers/how-do-i-file-for-a-homestead-exemption-in-kendall-county`

> File with the Kendall Appraisal District at kendallad.org, not Bexar CAD. Boerne is in Kendall County. You must have owned and occupied the home as your principal residence on January 1, and the application is due before May 1. There is no fee to file directly with the district.

### [How do I protest my property taxes in Texas?](./how-do-i-protest-my-property-taxes-in-texas.md)

`/answers/how-do-i-protest-my-property-taxes-in-texas`

> File a written notice of protest with your appraisal district by May 15, or 30 days after the district mails your notice of appraised value, whichever is later. Comptroller Form 50-132 works. The appraisal review board must give at least 15 days notice of the hearing, which you may attend in person, by phone, by video or by affidavit.

### [What is an ag exemption in Texas, and what are rollback taxes?](./what-is-an-ag-exemption-in-texas-and-what-are-rollback-taxes.md)

`/answers/what-is-an-ag-exemption-in-texas-and-what-are-rollback-taxes`

> An ag exemption is not an exemption. It is a special appraisal that values qualifying land on its agricultural productivity instead of market value. Changing the use triggers a rollback: three years of additional tax equal to the difference between what was paid on ag value and what would have been paid on market value.

### [What is the 10 percent homestead cap in Texas?](./what-is-the-10-percent-homestead-cap-in-texas.md)

`/answers/what-is-the-10-percent-homestead-cap-in-texas`

> Once a Texas home has a homestead exemption, the appraisal district cannot raise its net appraised value more than 10 percent per year, no matter how much the market moves. The cap does not transfer to a buyer. It resets to market value after a sale, which is why a new owner's tax bill often jumps.

### [What is the Texas disabled veteran property tax exemption?](./texas-disabled-veteran-property-tax-exemption.md)

`/answers/texas-disabled-veteran-property-tax-exemption`

> Texas gives disabled veterans a fixed dollar exemption off one property's appraised value, scaled to the VA disability rating and starting at $5,000 for a 10-29% rating. A veteran rated 100% disabled, or determined individually unemployable, gets a total exemption on their residence homestead under Tax Code 11.131.

### [What is the Texas homestead exemption in 2026?](./what-is-the-texas-homestead-exemption.md)

`/answers/what-is-the-texas-homestead-exemption`

> The Texas homestead exemption removes part of your home's value from property taxation. For 2026, school districts must exempt $140,000 of a residence homestead's value, up from $100,000 after voters approved Proposition 13 in November 2025. You must own and occupy the home as your principal residence on January 1.

### [What is the Texas over-65 property tax exemption and school tax ceiling?](./texas-over-65-property-tax-exemption-and-school-tax-ceiling.md)

`/answers/texas-over-65-property-tax-exemption-and-school-tax-ceiling`

> Texas homeowners 65 or older get an additional $60,000 school district homestead exemption on top of the general $140,000, plus a school tax ceiling that freezes school taxes at the amount for the year they qualified. Moving transfers the ceiling as a percentage, not a dollar amount, and only if you file for it.

### [Why did my Texas property taxes go up?](./why-did-my-texas-property-taxes-go-up.md)

`/answers/why-did-my-texas-property-taxes-go-up`

> Your tax bill is appraised value times the combined rate of every taxing unit that covers your property, minus exemptions. It rises when the appraisal rises, when any single entity raises its rate, when an exemption is lost or was never filed, or when the 10 percent homestead cap resets to market value after a sale.

## Investing

Short-term rentals, 1031 exchanges, BRRRR and the Texas-specific constraints that break playbooks imported from other states. No occupancy rates, ADRs, cap rates or rent figures appear on these pages; none could be sourced, and an invented one is worse than none.

### [Can an HOA ban short-term rentals in Texas?](./can-an-hoa-ban-short-term-rentals-in-texas.md)

`/answers/can-an-hoa-ban-short-term-rentals-in-texas`

> Often yes. Texas has no statewide short-term rental preemption statute, and an HOA declaration is a private contract that can be amended to restrict or prohibit short-term rentals. Tarr v. Timberwood Park (Tex. 2018) held only that a bare residential-purposes-only covenant does not by itself bar short stays.

### [How are rental properties taxed differently from a homestead in Texas?](./how-are-rental-properties-taxed-differently-in-texas.md)

`/answers/how-are-rental-properties-taxed-differently-in-texas`

> A Texas rental gets no residence homestead exemption and no 10 percent homestead appraisal cap. It is instead covered by the separate 20 percent circuit breaker on non-homestead property under Tax Code Section 23.231, which applies to tax years 2024 through 2026 and expires December 31, 2026 unless the Legislature renews it.

### [Is Boerne, Texas good for short-term rentals?](./is-boerne-good-for-short-term-rentals.md)

`/answers/is-boerne-good-for-short-term-rentals`

> Boerne allows short-term rentals under Ordinance No. 2023-09, so the real question is what caps the return. The binding constraints are a 200-foot block-face separation in residential zones, occupancy of two per bedroom plus two, a ban on events, 13 percent hotel occupancy tax, and a $500,000 liability insurance minimum.

### [What is a 1031 exchange?](./what-is-a-1031-exchange.md)

`/answers/what-is-a-1031-exchange`

> A 1031 exchange lets an owner defer capital gain on real property held for productive use in a trade or business or for investment by exchanging it for like-kind real property. You have 45 days to identify replacement property in writing and 180 days to receive it. A personal residence does not qualify.

### [What is a Texas cash-out refinance under Section 50(a)(6)?](./what-is-a-texas-cash-out-refinance-50a6.md)

`/answers/what-is-a-texas-cash-out-refinance-50a6`

> A Texas cash-out refinance is a home equity loan under Article XVI, Section 50(a)(6) of the Texas Constitution. All loans against the homestead combined cannot exceed 80 percent of fair market value, the loan cannot close until 12 days after the later of application or notice, and fees are capped at 2 percent.

### [What is the BRRRR method, and does it work in Texas?](./what-is-the-brrrr-method-and-does-it-work-in-texas.md)

`/answers/what-is-the-brrrr-method-and-does-it-work-in-texas`

> BRRRR is buy, rehab, rent, refinance, repeat. It works in Texas, with one constitutional catch: if the property is the borrower's homestead, the refinance is a Section 50(a)(6) loan capped at 80 percent loan-to-value with a 12-day waiting period. A rental held as an investment is not the homestead and is not governed by 50(a)(6).

## Financing

Loan limits, program structure and the Texas homestead lending rules. **No credit score minimums, down payment percentages, funding fee rates, MIP or PMI rates, DTI ratios or interest rates appear anywhere in this group.** None of them could be traced to a primary source, and the honest structural explanation is the more useful answer anyway.

### [Conventional vs. FHA vs. VA loan in Texas: which one should I use?](./conventional-vs-fha-vs-va-loan-in-texas.md)

`/answers/conventional-vs-fha-vs-va-loan-in-texas`

> Conventional loans follow the 2026 conforming limit of $832,750 for one unit. FHA uses a nationwide floor of $541,287 and a ceiling of $1,249,125, set by county. VA loans have no loan limit for a veteran with full entitlement, and the funding fee is waived for service-connected disability compensation. Ask a lender to price all three.

### [Do I need flood insurance in Boerne, Texas?](./do-i-need-flood-insurance-in-boerne.md)

`/answers/do-i-need-flood-insurance-in-boerne`

> If the property is in a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area and you are using a federally backed loan, the lender will require flood insurance. Outside that zone it is optional but not unnecessary — a map line is not a risk assessment. Boerne is in Kendall County; get a flood quote during the option period.

### [How much down payment do I need to buy a house in Texas?](./how-much-down-payment-do-i-need-in-texas.md)

`/answers/how-much-down-payment-do-i-need-in-texas`

> How much you put down depends on the loan program, whether the home will be your homestead or an investment property, and what your lender's overlay requires. Conventional, FHA and VA each set different minimums, and a veteran with full entitlement has no VA loan limit. Only a lender preapproval gives you a real number.

### [What are the 2026 conforming and FHA loan limits in Kendall County, Texas?](./what-are-the-2026-loan-limits-in-kendall-county.md)

`/answers/what-are-the-2026-loan-limits-in-kendall-county`

> For 2026 the conforming one-unit baseline is $832,750, with a high-cost ceiling of $1,249,125. FHA's nationwide one-unit floor is $541,287 and ceiling $1,249,125. Kendall County sits in the San Antonio-New Braunfels MSA, whose 2026 FHA one-unit limit is reported at $557,750 — confirm it on HUD's lookup. VA has no limit on full entitlement.

### [What credit score do I need to buy a house in Texas?](./what-credit-score-do-i-need-to-buy-a-house-in-texas.md)

`/answers/what-credit-score-do-i-need-to-buy-a-house-in-texas`

> There is no single number. Each loan program sets an agency minimum, and then the individual lender adds its own stricter overlay — the overlay is what you actually have to meet. Score also prices the loan and the mortgage insurance, not just approval. Get a real preapproval from a lender who pulls credit and reads your income documents.

### [What is the VA funding fee, and who is exempt?](./what-is-the-va-funding-fee-and-who-is-exempt.md)

`/answers/what-is-the-va-funding-fee-and-who-is-exempt`

> The VA funding fee is a one-time fee paid to the VA on most VA-guaranteed home loans, charged as a percentage of the loan amount. Veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability are exempt, as are several other groups. ## Relocation

For buyers arriving from California, Austin or out of state: what changes, what the tax trade actually is, and the Texas mechanics that surprise people who have bought houses before — just not here.

### [Does Texas really have no state income tax, and what do I pay instead?](./does-texas-have-no-state-income-tax.md)

`/answers/does-texas-have-no-state-income-tax`

> Yes. Texas has no state individual income tax. It funds local government mostly through property tax, and sales tax runs 6.25% state plus local add-ons to a maximum 8.25%, which is what Boerne charges. Texas property is reappraised at market value annually, subject to a 10% homestead cap.

### [What should I know about moving from Austin to Boerne?](./moving-from-austin-to-boerne.md)

`/answers/moving-from-austin-to-boerne`

> Boerne is in Kendall County, not Travis County: a different appraisal district (Kendall Appraisal District), a different school district (Boerne ISD), and a different water picture. It sits about 31 miles northwest of San Antonio on Interstate 10, a 30 to 45 minute drive downtown depending on traffic.

### [What should I know about moving from California to the Texas Hill Country?](./moving-from-california-to-the-texas-hill-country.md)

`/answers/moving-from-california-to-the-texas-hill-country`

> Texas has no state individual income tax, and it funds local government largely through property tax instead. Texas property is reappraised at market value every year, unlike California under Proposition 13. In Boerne, Boerne ISD's 2025 rate was 1.0109 and the City of Boerne's was 0.4716 per $100 of taxable value.

### [What surprises out-of-state buyers about buying a house in Texas?](./what-surprises-out-of-state-buyers-about-texas-real-estate.md)

`/answers/what-surprises-out-of-state-buyers-about-texas-real-estate`

> The biggest surprises are the option period, which lets a Texas buyer terminate for any reason; that no attorney is required and the title company runs closing; that title insurance premiums are set by the state, so they are identical everywhere; and that annual reappraisal replaces a state income tax.

## How these pages are built

Each file carries YAML frontmatter with `question`, `slug`, `short_answer`, `meta_description`, `category`, `target_keywords`, `related_questions`, `sources`, `schema_type: FAQPage` and `last_reviewed`.

The `short_answer` is the extract block. It is 40-60 words, it answers the question completely with no surrounding context, and it appears in three places verbatim: the frontmatter, the first paragraph of the body, and the first `acceptedAnswer` in the JSON-LD. That repetition is deliberate — it is the passage an assistant lifts.

## Standing editorial rules

1. **Boerne is Kendall County.** San Antonio ordinances do not govern it, and the appraisal district is Kendall AD, not Bexar CAD.
2. **No number appears without a source.** Where a figure could not be traced, the sentence is written without it and the gap is logged in NEEDS-VERIFICATION.md.
3. **Nothing is invented about the agent.** No testimonials, closing counts, years in business, awards or client anecdotes appear anywhere.
4. **The license line stops at legal and tax advice.** Pages explain the mechanism in full, then name the right professional — Texas real estate attorney, CPA, lender, appraisal district, City of Boerne Planning Department, licensed inspector, surveyor, insurance agent.
5. **Volatile figures are not hardcoded.** Median prices, drought stages and anything that moves monthly are attributed with a date or omitted.
