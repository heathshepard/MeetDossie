## Verified with a caveat worth knowing

### 4. $140,000 school homestead exemption — effective tax year
The pages state the $140,000 school district exemption and cite the Texas Comptroller (Tax Code
11.13(b)) and Kendall Appraisal District's adopted exemption schedule, **which shows $140,000 for
tax year 2025.**

Several secondary sources describe Prop 13 / SB 4 (approved November 2025) as raising the exemption
from $100,000 to $140,000 "effective for the 2026 tax year." That conflicts with KCAD's own 2025
certified roll and 2025 exemption schedule, both of which apply $140,000 to tax year 2025 — which
is consistent with SB 4 having been made contingent on the November 2025 election and retroactive
to January 1, 2025.

The pages avoid the conflict by citing what the appraisal district actually published rather than
the effective-date claim. **If anyone asks, KCAD's roll is the stronger source.** Still worth a
call to KCAD to confirm the framing before someone challenges it.

### 5. Boerne ISD 1.0109 rate and the VATRE
Confirmed by comparing two versions of the same KCAD document:
- The September 2025 version reads `*1.0109` with the footnote "Pending Voter Approval — Election
  Date 11/4/2025."
- The November 2025 version reads `1.0109` with no asterisk.

Combined with Community Impact's November 18, 2025 canvass coverage, the rate is final. Good
evidence, but it is an inference from a document revision rather than a direct statement. If Heath
wants belt-and-braces, Boerne ISD's business office can confirm in one call.

### 6. City of Boerne STR ordinance — fee currency
Ordinance 2023-09 sets the permit fee at $200 and the inspection fee at $100, but its own language
says fees apply "on the effective date of this ordinance, or as subsequently amended by the city
fee ordinance." Search results also surfaced a later agenda item referencing this ordinance
("2024-403 - STR Ordinance No. 2023-09"), suggesting subsequent council action.

Page 09 tells readers explicitly to confirm current fees with the Planning Department at
(830) 248-1501. **Someone should make that call once and update the page with the confirmed
current fee schedule, plus check whether Chapter 23 has been amended since 2023.**
