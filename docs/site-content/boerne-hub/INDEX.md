## Why this set, versus the eight originally scoped

All eight requested pages are here (01-08). Two were added:

**09 — Short-Term Rental Rules.** This is the strongest single authority play in the set. It is a
high-intent commercial query, the competing content is wrong (most of it imports San Antonio's
ordinance), and the page quotes the actual ordinance with section numbers. It is also the topic
where being right is most valuable, since the errors are expensive for the reader.

**10 — Buying Land and Acreage.** Boerne's genuine differentiation from every other San Antonio
suburb is land, and land is the segment with the highest transaction value and the thinnest
correct information online. It also carries Heath's land-development background naturally,
which no competing agent can copy.

## Fact base used across all pages

Every statistic in these pages traces to one of:

- **Kendall Appraisal District** — 2025 adopted tax rates and exemptions; per-entity rate history
  PDFs; 2025 Certified Totals (as of Supplement 47, 12/3/2025); 2026 Updated Property Tax
  Information; Kendall County Ag Use Manual.
- **Bexar County** — 2025 Official Tax Rates & Exemptions.
- **City of Boerne** — Current Tax Rates page; Short-Term Rentals page; Ordinance No. 2023-09 (full text).
- **Kendall County** — County Engineer verification letter, March 4, 2026; Onsite Sewage; Dark Sky
  Outdoor Lighting.
- **City of Fair Oaks Ranch** — Property Tax Servicing page.
- **Boerne ISD** — Attendance Zones and Boundaries.
- **Boerne Star / Community Impact** — TEA ratings coverage, VATRE canvass, county and city budget
  adoption, population estimates, utility rate changes.
- **Texas Comptroller** — property tax exemptions.
- **SABOR MLS** — market figures supplied by Heath, labeled "as of September 2026, per SABOR MLS."

No client testimonials, closing counts, sales volume or awards appear anywhere in these pages —
there is no verified data for any of those. No transaction is referenced by name.
