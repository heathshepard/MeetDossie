# Boerne Property Taxes Explained

Everything below comes from the Kendall Appraisal District's own published rate and exemption schedules and its 2025 certified appraisal roll. No estimates, no "approximately."

**Start here:** Boerne is in **Kendall County**. It is not in Bexar County and it is not part of the City of San Antonio. If you're reading a property tax article about "San Antonio area" rates, exemptions, or ordinances, it does not describe your Boerne bill.

## Who taxes a Boerne property

Adopted rates for tax year 2025, per Kendall Appraisal District:

| City of Boerne | CBN | 0.4716 | — | — | — |

All entities offer the state disabled veteran partial exemptions: **DV1 $5,000, DV2 $7,500, DV3 $10,000, DV4 $12,000**.

A tax ceiling freeze is offered by the City of Boerne, Boerne ISD, Comfort ISD and Kendall County for a qualifying homestead with an over-65 or disabled person exemption.

**The single biggest lever on your Boerne tax bill is whether the parcel is inside city limits.** On the median area sale price of about $602,600 (as of September 2026, per SABOR MLS), the City of Boerne's 0.4716 rate is **$2,841.86 a year** — about $237 a month of escrow.

## The exemption fact that surprises everyone

**Neither the City of Boerne nor Kendall County offers a general residence homestead exemption.** Look at the table above: the homestead column is blank for both. Kendall County offers $10,000 for over-65 and disabled persons. The City of Boerne offers a freeze for qualifying over-65 and disabled homesteads but no general homestead reduction.

The only general homestead exemption on your Boerne bill is the state-mandated **$140,000 school district exemption**, reflected at both Boerne ISD and Comfort ISD on the appraisal district's published schedule.

That is a real difference from Bexar County, where the county, the city, the hospital district, the community college district and the river authority each offer their own local homestead exemption. If you're relocating from San Antonio, don't assume your exemption savings scale.

### What the homestead exemption is actually worth here

$140,000 × 1.0109% = **$1,415.26 a year**. That's it — the school district piece is the whole benefit.

File it anyway, for a second reason that matters more over time: the homestead exemption is what starts your 10% annual appraisal cap.

## Exemptions, one by one

### General residence homestead — $140,000 (school districts)

Texas Tax Code 11.13(b) requires school districts to provide a $140,000 residence homestead exemption (Texas Comptroller). Kendall Appraisal District's adopted exemption schedule reflects $140,000 at Boerne ISD and Comfort ISD for tax year 2025, up from $100,000 in 2024 and $40,000 in 2022.

A homestead is generally the home and land used as the owner's principal residence on January 1 of the tax year. Important for buyers: a person who acquires property after January 1 may receive the residence homestead exemption for the applicable portion of that tax year **immediately upon qualifying**, if the preceding owner did not already receive the same exemption for that year (Kendall Appraisal District, March 2026).

The general deadline for filing an exemption application is before May 1 (Texas Comptroller). **It is free.** If you get a letter offering to file it for you for a fee, throw it away.

### Over-65 and disabled person

An additional **$60,000** school district exemption under Tax Code 11.13(c), plus **$10,000** from Kendall County. The City of Boerne offers no additional amount but does offer a freeze.

The **tax ceiling freeze** is the more valuable piece for most seniors. Once set, further rate or value increases don't raise your tax for that entity. Boerne ISD's 2025 roll shows **4,772 over-65 accounts under a ceiling** and the City of Boerne's shows 1,466 (Kendall Appraisal District, 2025 Certified Totals).

The freeze does not transfer to a buyer. If you're buying from a senior under a freeze, your bill will be materially different from theirs.

### Disabled veteran

Partial exemptions by disability rating: DV1 $5,000, DV2 $7,500, DV3 $10,000, DV4 $12,000, offered by every entity in the county.

Separately, Texas law provides a **100 percent homestead exemption for 100 percent disabled veterans and their surviving spouses**, as well as for surviving spouses of service members killed or fatally injured in the line of duty (Kendall Appraisal District, March 2026).

This is a significant feature of the Boerne market. Boerne ISD's 2025 certified roll shows **713 accounts** under the 100% disabled veteran homestead exemption, worth **$354.6 million** in exempted value, plus 37 surviving-spouse accounts worth $10.2 million. If you're a disabled veteran considering Boerne, this exemption should be part of your math from the beginning.

### Agricultural and wildlife management valuation

This is the largest single tax feature in Kendall County and it gets almost no coverage.

Boerne ISD's 2025 roll shows **$1.98 billion in agricultural market value** appraised at **$9.2 million** under productivity valuation — a productivity loss of **$1.97 billion** (Kendall Appraisal District, 2025 Certified Totals). A great deal of this county is still ranch land carrying a 1-d-1 open-space valuation.

Qualifying, per the Kendall County Ag Use Manual:
- The land must have been used **principally for agriculture for five of the preceding seven years**.
- **Cow/calf**: minimum of 3 calves and 10 acres.
- **Sheep**: ewes plus one ram on 10 acres. **Goats**: 6 does and a buck, minimum 10 acres.
- **Exotics**: minimum 10 acres with wire mesh fencing.
- **Beekeeping**: not less than 5 nor more than 20 acres, minimum of 6 hives.
- **Orchard, vineyard or market garden**: minimum 3 acres. Family gardens do not qualify.
- **Wildlife management**: a minimum of three approved practices, with the land already qualified under 1-d-1.

Stocking rates are set by range type: Improved Grass Range 1 animal unit per 5 acres; Native Range I 1 AU per 10 acres; Native Range II 1 AU per 25 acres; Native Range III (oak) 1 AU per 35 acres; Native Range III (cedar) 1 AU per 50 acres.

**Buyer warning:** if you buy land with an ag valuation and don't continue a qualifying use, you can trigger a rollback of back taxes. Decide before closing whether you intend to maintain it, and get your plan in front of the appraisal district.

## The caps

### 10% homestead appraisal cap

Once a residence homestead exemption is in place, appraised value can rise no more than 10% per year plus the value of new improvements. In Boerne ISD alone, this removed **$543.2 million** of value from the 2025 roll.

It applies only to homesteads, and it resets when the property sells. That reset is the single most common source of first-year tax shock for buyers here.

### The 20% non-homestead circuit breaker (Tax Code 23.231)

Texas SB 2 (2023) created a temporary 20% annual appraisal cap on non-homestead real property at or below an indexed value threshold — $5,000,000 for 2024, $5,160,000 for 2025 and $5,320,000 for 2026. It applies to rental homes, commercial property and other investment real estate. It **expires December 31, 2026 unless the legislature renews it.**

In Boerne ISD, it removed $68.6 million of value from the 2025 roll — smaller than the homestead cap, but real if you own rentals here.

If you hold non-homestead property in Kendall County, watch this. Its expiration would be a meaningful change to your 2027 tax exposure.

### Deferrals

Two exist and both are underused:

- **Residence homestead tax deferral**: pay tax on 105% of the preceding year's appraised value plus taxes on new improvements; the remainder is deferred with interest accruing at 8% per year.
- **Over-65, disabled, or disabled veteran deferral**: defer current and delinquent taxes as long as you own and live in the home, with interest accruing at 5% per year.

Both postpone taxes. Neither cancels them. Affidavits are available at the appraisal district.

## How to protest

### The deadline

**May 15, or 30 days after your Notice of Appraised Value was mailed, whichever is later.** Your notice will state the deadline for your property. If it falls on a weekend or legal holiday it moves to the next business day.

Kendall Appraisal District accepts protests through its online portal at **eprotest.kendallad.org**, and by mail, in person or drop box.

### The steps

**1. Get the district's evidence.** You are entitled to the information the appraisal district used to value your property. Ask for it before your hearing, not at it.

**2. Request an informal meeting.** Property owners can request an informal meeting with appraisal district staff to try to resolve the dispute before an ARB hearing. A large share of protests are settled here, and it is far less work than a hearing.

**3. The ARB hearing.** A written protest must identify the owner, the property, and the reasons for dissatisfaction. The district publishes its ARB hearing procedures each year.

**4. If you're not satisfied:** binding arbitration, an appeal to district court, or the State Office of Administrative Hearings. There is also **limited binding arbitration**, which compels the ARB or chief appraiser to comply with a procedural requirement.

### What actually works

- **Unequal appraisal** beats market-value arguments more often than owners expect. If comparable properties are appraised lower, that's a statutory ground.
- **Condition evidence wins.** Photographs of a failing roof, foundation movement, an outdated kitchen, or deferred maintenance are concrete. Contractor bids are better than photographs.
- **A recent purchase price below the appraised value** is strong evidence. Bring the closing statement.
- **Bad property characteristics in the district's records** — wrong square footage, a pool that doesn't exist, a finished-out space that isn't — are the easiest wins available and they're common. Pull your property record card at esearch.kendallad.org and read it line by line.
- **Protest every year.** The cap protects you from a 30% increase; it does not stop a 10% increase compounding annually.

## Common questions

**Is Boerne in Bexar County?**
No. Boerne is in Kendall County. Property is appraised by the Kendall Appraisal District. San Antonio and Bexar County rates, exemptions and ordinances do not apply.

**Why is my tax bill so much higher than the seller's was?**
Because the 10% homestead appraisal cap and any over-65 ceiling freeze reset when the property sells. The property is reappraised to market — typically the price you just paid. Never underwrite off the prior owner's bill.

**Does Kendall County offer a homestead exemption?**
Not a general one. Kendall County offers $10,000 for over-65 and disabled persons. The City of Boerne offers a freeze for qualifying over-65 and disabled homesteads but no general homestead reduction. Your general homestead exemption here comes entirely from the school district.

**When is the homestead exemption deadline?**
The general deadline for filing an exemption application is before May 1. It is free to file.

**When are taxes due?**
Tax statements go out in the fall and become delinquent February 1 of the following year. Confirm exact dates with the Kendall County Tax Assessor-Collector.

**Should I hire a protest company?**
Sometimes. They typically take a percentage of your savings. On a straightforward residential protest with good condition evidence, most owners can do it themselves through the informal meeting. On a complex property, an unequal-appraisal argument, or if you simply won't do it otherwise, a professional beats not protesting.
