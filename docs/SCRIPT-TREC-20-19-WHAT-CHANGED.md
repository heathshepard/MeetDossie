## CARD ORDER — and why

**Card 1 is ¶7.I.** I agree with the coordinator's read, and the reason is sharper than "biggest
change": every other item on this list costs an agent *paperwork*. ¶7.I is the only one that
costs them a *deal*. It creates a live termination right that is not bounded by the option
period, which means an agent can do everything right through the option and still lose the file
in week four. That's the §2 opening the standard asks for — a specific transaction nightmare an
agent immediately recognizes.

Card 2 is the orientation card, placed second on purpose. Leading with "here's the form number"
would be exactly the "today I want to talk about" open the standard bans. Leading with the scare
and *then* answering "wait, am I even on the right form?" is the order an agent's head actually
works in.

## CARD 1 — "What's this new water disclosure, and why does it outlive the option period?"

`[SCREEN: page 5 of the blank 20-19, ¶7.I, scroll slowly through the three boxes]`

**Beats:**
- Brand new paragraph, no analog in 20-18 at all — it's at **¶7.I**, inside PROPERTY CONDITION, not buried in the title notices.
- Three boxes, check one only: received it / hasn't received it with a day count / not required.
- The middle box is the dangerous one. No delivery = buyer terminates **any time before closing**, money back. Late delivery = buyer gets **7 days from receipt**, any reason, money back. Neither is bounded by the option period.
- Box three isn't a freebie — **all five** conditions have to be true, and you have to name the water provider in the blank.
- Practitioner note: on SAWS in a platted subdivision it's ten seconds. On Hill Country acreage with a well it's a conversation. And "in use **or not in use**" means a capped, forgotten well still kills box three.

**MUST BE EXACT:**
- It is **¶7.I**. Not ¶6.E. Not ¶7.J.
- New in **20-19**; it did not exist in 20-18 in any form.
- The terminate-any-time right is in **7.I(2)**, and it runs **"at any time prior to the closing."**
- Late delivery gives **7 days** from receipt, "for any reason."
- Box **7.I(3)** requires **all five** sub-conditions, (a) through (e).
- Say **"the Seller's Water Disclosure published by TREC"** — the contract prints no form number for it.

**MUST NOT SAY:**
- **Do not say a form number for it.** 61-0 is corroborated but *not* first-party verified. Name only.
- Don't say it's "in paragraph 6" — that was in a bad internal doc and it's wrong.

## CARD 2 — "How do I know I'm even on the right form?"

`[SCREEN: bottom-right corner of page 1, zoom on "TREC NO. 20-19"; then the footer date stamp]`

**Beats:**
- Bottom right corner of every page. If it says 20-18, it's last year's form.
- Page count is the fastest tell across the room — 20-19 is 12 pages, 20-18 was 11.
- Effective July 1. New offers on or after that date go on 20-19.
- Mid-transaction on a 20-18? You stay on 20-18. You don't re-paper an executed contract.
- Small one that trips people: there is no paragraph 24. The form stops at 23, and the effective-date block after it is unnumbered.

**MUST BE EXACT:**
- **TREC No. 20-19**, footer of every page.
- Footer revision stamp reads **05-04-2026**; effective date is **July 1, 2026**.
- **12 pages.** 20-18 was **11**.
- Paragraphs run **1 through 23**. The execution block on **page 10** is **unnumbered** — cite it as "the execution block on page 10," never "¶24."
- It replaces **20-18** (rev 11-04-2024).

**MUST NOT SAY:**
- Never "paragraph 24." It does not exist in 20-19 *or* 20-18.
- Don't say 20-19 is "optional" or "voluntary" — it's the promulgated form.

## CARD 3 — "Where did the seller contribution to the buyer's agent go?"

`[SCREEN: split — 20-18 ¶12.A(1)(b) struck on the left, 20-19 ¶12.B on the right]`

**Beats:**
- It moved. It is not in 12-A anymore — that's the muscle memory that's going to cost people.
- There's now a whole paragraph 12-B called Brokerage Compensation, and it runs **both directions**: seller toward the buyer's broker, and buyer toward the seller's broker.
- Each one is an enabling box plus a dollars-or-percent choice. Three boxes per row.
- The bolded sentence is the one to read out loud: these contributions apply to, and **do not change**, what each party already owes their own broker under their separate agreement.
- Practitioner note: a seller contribution is not a commission agreement. Buyer-rep says 2.5%, seller contributes 2%, the client owes the rest. Say that at the table, not at closing.
- And 12-A(1)(b) — the general seller concession line — now explicitly **excludes** brokerage compensation. Two buckets. Don't stack them into one number.
- The old ¶12.B, which let a party terminate if an expense ran over a stated amount, is **deleted**. It didn't move. ¶12.C is a narrower thing about FHA/VA non-allowables.

**MUST BE EXACT:**
- Old location: **¶12.A(1)(b)** in 20-18. New location: **¶12.B** in 20-19.
- **12.B(1)** = Seller pays toward what Buyer owes Buyer's broker. **12.B(2)** = Buyer pays toward what Seller owes Seller's broker.
- 20-18's old ¶12.B (expense-overage termination right) is **struck, not relocated**.
- **¶12.C** is EXPENSE LIMITATION — governmental loan program prohibited charges only.

**MUST NOT SAY:**
- Don't say the old expense-overage termination right "moved to 12-C." It was deleted. 12-C does something else.
- Don't quote a commission percentage as standard. Brokerage compensation is not set by law and is fully negotiable — that's the form's own first sentence in 12-B.

**Runtime: 1:15**

## CARD 4 — "What happened to paragraph 8?"

`[SCREEN: ¶8, page 5–6]`

**Beats:**
- Used to be "Brokers and Sales Agents" with an A and a B. Now it's "Broker or Sales Agent Disclosure," one block, no letters.
- The old 8-B — "all obligations for payment of brokers' fees are in separate written agreements" — is gone. That idea lives in 12-B now.
- What's left is the licensee-as-a-party disclosure, and it's broader than most agents think: you, spouse, parent, child, an entity you own more than 10% of, a trust you're tied to.
- And the timing word is the one that matters — **before** entering into a contract of sale. Not at closing, not in the MLS remarks.

**MUST BE EXACT:**
- **¶8**, renamed from "BROKERS AND SALES AGENTS" to **"BROKER OR SALES AGENT DISCLOSURE."**
- Old **8.B deleted**. The disclosure text itself survives verbatim.
- The ownership threshold is **more than 10%**.
- Notice must be **in writing, before entering into a contract of sale**.

**MUST NOT SAY:**
- Don't say the disclosure requirement itself changed. It didn't — only the heading and the deletion of 8.B.

**Runtime: 0:35**

## CARD 5 — "Where did the required-notices box in paragraph 6 go?"

`[SCREEN: ¶6.E ending at (11); then cut to ¶22's Statutory Disclosures group]`

**Beats:**
- Title notices in 6-E used to run to twelve. Now they stop at eleven, at the mold remediation certificate.
- Old (12), Required Notices, is deleted from 6-E. It moved into paragraph 22 under a new heading.
- Same trap, new address — and there's now a bolded warning under it that failure to provide statutory disclosures may give the buyer remedies or rights to terminate.
- If you've been checking that box out of habit at the top of the form, it isn't there anymore.

**MUST BE EXACT:**
- 20-18 ¶6.E ran **(1) through (12)**. 20-19 ¶6.E **ends at (11)**, Certificate of Mold Remediation.
- Items (1)–(11) are **unchanged and unrenumbered**.
- Old (12) moved into **¶22**, under **"Statutory Disclosures and Notices."**

**MUST NOT SAY:**
- Don't imply the other title notices were renumbered. They weren't.

## CARD 6 — "Why can't I find the addendum box I always check?"

`[SCREEN: ¶22, page 9, scroll the five group headers]`

**Beats:**
- The addenda checklist isn't one long list anymore. Five named groups: Financial, Leases, Additional Tests and Reports, Statutory Disclosures and Notices, Other.
- If you find a box by where it sits on the page, stop doing that and read the headers.
- Two boxes are new to the list: the VA entitlement restoration one, and Non-Realty Items.
- One is gone as a standalone: the improvement-district assessment notice. It folded into that district-notices line we just talked about.
- And the standing rule on 22, which hasn't changed: every box you check has a document attached, every document attached has a box checked. Mismatches are the number one thing compliance kicks back.

**MUST BE EXACT:**
- Five groups: **Financial · Leases · Additional Tests and Reports · Statutory Disclosures and Notices · Other.**
- Added to the list: **"Addendum for Release of Liability on Assumed Loan and/or Restoration of Seller's VA Entitlement"** and **"Non-Realty Items Addendum."**
- Removed as a standalone checkbox: the **improvement district assessment** notice — folded into the district-notices line.
- ¶22 names every addendum **by name only. No form numbers appear anywhere in it.**

**MUST NOT SAY:**
- **Do not quote addendum form numbers on this card.** The contract prints none. Names only.

## CARD 7 — "Anything else I'd miss on a skim?"

*The rapid-fire card. Four small ones, fast, no lingering.*

`[SCREEN: quick cuts — ¶20, then ¶21, then ¶17, then the page 11 broker block]`

**Beats:**
- Paragraph 20 was Federal Requirements, now it's Governmental Requirements, and it's lettered. FIRPTA is 20-A. New 20-B: both sides promptly sign whatever escrow needs for governmental reporting.
- Paragraph 21, notices: fax is gone as a delivery method. Mail, hand delivery, overnight courier, electronic transmission. And it's now notices "from one party **or their agent**" to the other.
- Paragraph 17, attorney's fees: Listing Broker and Other Broker became Seller's broker and Buyer's broker.
- Page 11, the broker page: the old line about the listing broker having agreed to pay the other broker a fee is deleted. That's the post-settlement cleanup finishing. Compensation lives in 12-B now and nowhere else.

**MUST BE EXACT:**
- **¶20** renamed FEDERAL → **GOVERNMENTAL REQUIREMENTS**; FIRPTA is now **20.A**; **20.B is new**.
- **¶21** dropped **fax** — and only ¶21 did. Fax fields still exist in the ¶23 attorney blocks and on the page-12 receipts.
- **¶17** — "Listing Broker / Other Broker" → **"Seller's broker / Buyer's broker."**
- The deleted broker-page line is the **"pursuant to a previous, separate agreement"** compensation disclosure, on **page 11**.

**MUST NOT SAY:**
- Don't say "fax is gone from the contract." It's gone from ¶21 only.

## CARD 8 — "What did NOT change, that people think did?"

`[SCREEN: ¶3.B side by side, both forms identical; then ¶10.B in both]`

**Beats:**
- Paragraph 3-B financing boxes: still three. Third Party Financing, Loan Assumption, Seller Financing. Byte for byte identical to 20-18. There's a summary going around that says they got consolidated to two — that's wrong.
- Smart Devices at 10-B: **not new.** It was in 20-18, same text, same place. It's underused, but it didn't change.
- The legal holiday extension in 5-A-2 was already there in 20-18. Wording got tightened, that's it.
- Everything in paragraphs 2, 4, 9, 11, 13 through 16, 18, 19 and 23 is substantively the same.

**MUST BE EXACT:**
- **¶3.B still has THREE checkboxes.** They are unchanged.
- **¶10.B SMART DEVICES carried over from 20-18 and is NOT new.**
- **¶5.A(2)** Legal Holiday extension: present in 20-18, carried over.

**MUST NOT SAY — this card is where an on-camera error is most likely:**
- **Do NOT say Smart Devices is new in 20-19.** It is not. It's at ¶10.B in both forms with the same text. Calling it new is the error a 20-year agent catches and it costs the whole video's credibility.
- **Do NOT say ¶3.B was consolidated to two boxes.** That came from a bad internal summary. Three boxes, unchanged.

## CARD 9 — "So what do I actually do Monday?"

`[FACE — no document, straight to camera. This is the payoff card.]`

**Beats:**
- Pull a blank 20-19 and check the footer on the form your brokerage or your forms software is serving you. If it's 20-18, fix that before you write another offer.
- On every listing: get the Water Disclosure delivered, or get 7-I-3 elected correctly with the provider named. That closes the post-option exit.
- On every offer: 12-B is the compensation line now, not 12-A. Look at it once deliberately so it stops being a reflex.
- And read paragraph 22 with your eyes open, not from memory, until the new grouping is in your hands.

**MUST BE EXACT:**
- **¶7.I(3)** is the election that removes the exposure, and it requires naming the provider.
- Compensation is **¶12.B**, not ¶12.A.

**MUST NOT SAY:**
- Nothing that sounds like legal advice. This is what a practitioner does, not what a lawyer tells you to do.
