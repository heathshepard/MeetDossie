numbers in this niche are *bought*. A 5.1M-view video with 5 likes is a YouTube ad, not a hit. Once
you strip those out, the only organic winners in our lane are **long-form contract education on
small channels** — and the most-requested unserved thing in the entire lane is *an updated
paragraph-by-paragraph TREC 1-4 walkthrough*. Heath can make that this week.

## 2. The methodological finding that reframes everything

`like_count ÷ view_count` cleanly separates two populations, with nothing in between:

| Population | Like rate | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid / promoted** | 0.0001% – 0.07% | Carepatron 5.1M views → **5 likes**; BoldTrail 604k → **1 like**; Carepatron 486k → **0 likes**; PracticePanther Shorts 0.06-0.07% |
| **Organic** | 0.66% – 4.33% | Every TC educator, every agent coach, every TikTok winner |

There is no organic YouTube video anywhere in this research with a like rate under 0.6%, and no
paid one above 0.08%. So: **Carepatron's "655x its subscriber count" is an ad buy.** NextPatient's
"21,258x on 23 subscribers" is an ad buy. dotloop's and SkySlope's hidden like counts, zero
comments, and testimonial format put them in the same bucket.

This matters directly: if we copy the Carepatron/BoldTrail *format* and post it organically, we get
our current numbers. That format is a **paid creative**, and it only works with spend behind it.
It belongs in the $300 Meta test from the 09-18 doc (§5 there), not in the organic calendar.

## 3. Dissection of the top 8

Every "first 3 seconds" below is the **literal** first frames and the literal auto-caption
transcript, pulled with `yt-dlp` + `ffmpeg` on 2026-09-22 — not a description of a thumbnail.

### 3.1 Carepatron — "Welcome to Carepatron" (5.1M views, 40 s, 1.7 cuts/10 s) — PAID
- **Frame 0-3 s:** cinematic wide, teal-walled room; a woman in the foreground half-turned away from
  a family dinner table where two kids eat; warm practical light, shallow depth of field. **No text
  on screen. No logo. No product.**
- **Spoken, verbatim from 0:00.88:** *"You didn't become a practitioner to drown in admin, chase
  no-shows, or spend your evenings on notes."*
- Hook type: **negation-of-identity.** "You didn't become X to do Y."
- Face: yes, frame one, but it is a *hired actor in a scene*, not a founder to camera.
- Captions: none burned in. Audio: professional VO over score, no dialogue.
- CTA: last 4 s only — "Start for free today."
- Comments: **zero.** 5 likes on 5.1M views. Nothing to learn from the comments because there are none.

### 3.2 Carepatron — "Beautiful Practice Management Software" (1.39M, 47 s, 0.4 cuts/10 s) — PAID
- **Frame 0-3 s:** a woman alone on a blue couch at night, laptop on her knees, red coffee cup on
  the table, hand going to her forehead. A **single locked-off shot held for 12 seconds.**
- **Spoken from 0:00.24:** *"Still drowning in paperwork while trying to grow your practice?"*
- Hook type: **pain-state question**, asked over a silent visual of that exact pain.
- Structure worth stealing: pain (0-12 s) → product (12-31 s) → **social proof with a number**
  ("Over 50,000 practitioners trust…") → **a real customer's single line of dialogue** ("Carepatron
  saves me 3 hours every day") → CTA. That five-beat structure is the whole video.

### 3.3 PracticePanther — "Lawyers Should Be Lawyering (feat. Rick Hoffman)" (1.01M, 702 likes, 65 s) — PAID but genuinely liked
- **Frame 0-3 s:** black frame → rhythmic pencil-tapping sound → an office; a young associate drums
  on a desk. First words are **dialogue, not VO**: *"Hoff, seriously?"* … *"Hoffman!"* … *"Sorry,
  Mr Hoffman."*
- Hook type: **cold open into a scene mid-argument.** The viewer is dropped into a workplace joke
  with no setup. The brand is not mentioned for 22 seconds.
- Casting: Rick Hoffman playing his own *Suits* character type — the celebrity is the hook and it is
  named in the title.
- The VO line that carries the whole pitch, at 0:25.68: *"Your one job is hard enough. You don't
  need four or five more."*
- **What the comments reveal:** the only video in the paid set with real comments, and every one is
  about the *entertainment*, not the software. People forgive an ad that is a genuinely good sketch.

### 3.4 PracticePanther — "Wearing All the Hats" (637k, 30 s, **5.3 cuts/10 s** — the fastest in the set)
- **Frame 0-3 s:** wide static shot of a law office; a bald partner sits behind a desk buried in
  paper, boxes stacked on the floor and on a chair. Nobody speaks for the first beat.
- **Spoken from 0:00.00:** *"Running a law firm means wearing a lot of different hats."*
- Then rapid-fire dialogue, 16 cuts in 30 seconds: *"Hey Hoff, has the Campbell intake form been
  completed?" — "I'm sure it's around here somewhere." — "Well how about this pile?" — "…that's
  received, that's completed, not enough information, too much information, and español."*
- Hook type: **literal-metaphor sketch.** The abstract complaint ("too many hats") is dramatised as
  a physical absurdity (piles of paper).
- Punchline after the CTA at 0:27.80: *"It's a horror movie about a hot dog that attacks children."*
  A joke **after** the pitch, so the last thing you feel is amusement.

### 3.5 SkySlope — "Close More Deals with Less Work" (229k, 30 s, **0 cuts** — one continuous CGI shot) — PAID
- **Frame 0-3 s:** an astronaut walking across the lunar surface with Earth rising behind them.
  At ~1.5 s, grey text fades up and resolves to white: **"Navigating real estate compliance isn't
  easy."** At ~5 s: **"At SkySlope, we cut out / complexity"** (the word "complexity" in large bold).
- **No human face at all. No dialogue.** Music-and-text only; the "transcript" is on-screen copy.
- Hook type: **metaphor + typed claim.** This is the closest thing in the set to what Dossie
  currently produces — and it is a paid brand film from a company with a national sales team. It is
  the format we should be **least** tempted by.

### 3.6 NextPatient — "Finally, software that delivers" (489k on a **23-subscriber** channel, 19 s) — PAID
- **Spoken from 0:00.24, no preamble, no brand card:** *"If I had to give advice to a fellow
  practice manager who's considering NextPatient, I would say sign the paperwork and get started."*
  Then: *"Don't delay. You don't need to trial any more software. What they say it can do, it does.
  And they'll deliver that and more."*
- Hook type: **customer answering an unasked question**, filmed as a plain testimonial to camera.
- 19 seconds. One person. One room. No B-roll, no captions, no graphics.
- The channel has **23 subscribers and one purpose**: to host ad creative. That is what a 21,258x
  "ratio" actually means.

### 3.7 Inside Real Estate / BoldTrail — "Cory Lampman… How BoldTrail Transformed Their Brokerage" (604k, 1 like, 35 s) — PAID
- **Spoken from 0:00.00:** *"Without BoldTrail, I don't know how we would do what we do as a
  brokerage."* Named real customer, named real brokerage, straight to camera.
- Then specifics, fast: *"we can really see who's logging in, who's using the system, who's
  following up with leads… we can set up with multiple MLSs… each office can independently route
  leads."*
- Closes on a competitive claim: *"I don't know another system that does that."*
- The title **is** the structure: `[Name] from [Company] Shares How [Product] Transformed [Their
  Business]`. BoldTrail ran that exact template at least three times.

### 3.8 dotloop — "PARKS Realty wins with dotloop" (512k, 62 s, 2018) — PAID
- **Spoken from 0:02.08:** *"We have to adapt to where people are now with technology. Clients want
  things immediately, clients want things easily, clients want things on their phone — and dotloop
  provides all of that."*
- Hook type: **rule-of-three escalation** in the customer's own voice, then the product name as the
  resolution of the third beat.
- Eight years old and still the highest-viewed video on the channel. The transaction-software
  testimonial format is not new and has not been improved on since.

### Cross-cutting pattern across all 8
1. **Not one of them opens on a brand card, a logo, or a product name.** Ours does.
2. Five of eight open on a **human speaking within 1 second**. The two that don't (SkySlope, and
   PracticePanther's cold open) open on a *situation*.
3. Length: 19-65 s. Median 37 s. Ours is 33 s — length is the one thing we already have right.
4. Cuts/10 s ranges from **0 to 5.3**, and both extremes won. Cut rate is not the variable.
5. **Zero use burned-in karaoke captions.** (Note: these are all YouTube. The TikTok/IG winners in
   §0 do use them. Platform-dependent.)
6. Every one is about **the person's day**, not the software's features. The product appears at
   0:14, 0:12, 0:22, 0:19, 0:05, never (testimonial), 0:00 (customer names it), 0:12.

### 4c. The biggest unserved question — our content list
This is the deliverable that matters most, and it comes from real comments pulled 2026-09-22.

**Finding 1 — the TC lane's engaged audience is people who want to BECOME a TC, not agents who want
to buy one.** Of ~60 question-comments read across the three top TC videos, essentially all are
career questions: *"Is there an online school to learn how to become a TC from scratch? I have no
real estate experience"* (4 likes) · *"I'm a licensed agent transitioning into a TC… does anyone
recommend a platform?"* (5) · *"I went to a masterclass that teaches you to become a TC and scale a
TC business. The program is $5k! Wondering if 5k is worth it"* (6) · *"This is the training I've
been looking for. I know the steps to starting a business, but I needed the systems and routines
involved in the day to day work"* (11) · *"I'm 56 and in a nursing home in Las Vegas… searching for
a remote job I can do on my Android tablet."* **Exactly one** comment in the whole set was an agent
wanting to hire: *"Do you work in Florida? Can you be my transaction coordinator?!"* (2 likes).
That is Top Tier TC's business model, not ours — and it is why their ads run for 400-900 days
(§5). **Making "become a TC" content would grow an audience that cannot buy Dossie.**

**Finding 2 — the *agent* questions live under TREC contract videos, and they are all Dossie
features.** From Chandler Crouch's and Central Metro's comment threads, verbatim:
- *"Please do a new video with the current 1-4 please."* — top comment on a 75,897-view video, **and

**That list is the content calendar.** Every item is a question a real person typed, it is Texas-
or TREC-specific in most cases, Heath can answer all of them from memory, and each one maps to a

## 6. The emulation plan — five formats, tied to specific videos

Ranked by what Heath can shoot this week with what he already has. He has **18 feature-demo screen
recordings** in `Media/feature-demos/`, a cloned voice, a voice-clone source file, and **no face
footage of himself anywhere in `Media/`**. Every format below requires him on camera; that is the
binding constraint, and it is one phone and thirty minutes.

### Format 1 — "The updated TREC 1-4, one paragraph at a time" (long-form, the highest-confidence bet)
**Copying:** #18 Chandler Crouch, `A5nlRtkjQm0`, 75,897 views on 12,000 subs, 1.38% like rate,
**2 hours 13 minutes**, and the top comment is *"Please do a new video with the current 1-4
please."* Plus #21 Bren Brewer `FTJDzYDnyXM` (4.33% like rate on 2026 form changes), #12-14 (all
3.4-3.7% like rates on 6-33 minute TC long-form).

**What Heath's version is:** one screen-share of the current TREC 20-19 One to Four Family
Residential Contract, paragraph by paragraph, in his voice, with a real deal's facts on screen where
a clause matters. Two hours, or split into a numbered series the way Breckheimer split his into six.
He knows this material cold — the `acroform-field-names-lie` and
`feedback_verify-contract-elections-before-execution` memos exist because he has litigated these
exact paragraphs in production.

**Exact segments to record first, because they are the questions people actually asked:**
¶6C survey election ("WILL be amended to read shortages") · Third Party Financing Addendum property

executed date · the option-period clock and the `friday-execution-option-fee-trap` case · what
"time is of the essence" does and does not do to an extension.

**Why this is first:** zero production value required, it is the only format in this research with a
proven organic audience in our niche, the top request is literally unanswered, and the byproduct is
~20 short clips for everything below.

- **Anything requiring bought distribution posing as organic.** Carepatron, NextPatient, BoldTrail,

## 8. Method

- YouTube: `ytInitialData` via curl for search/channel pages; `yt-dlp -J --write-comments` for
  view/like/comment counts, durations, upload dates and comment text; `yt-dlp --write-auto-subs` for
  literal opening transcripts; `ffmpeg` frame extraction at n=0/15/45/90 for literal first frames
  and `select='gt(scene,0.25)'` for cut counts.
- TikTok: `__UNIVERSAL_DATA_FOR_REHYDRATION__` SSR JSON via curl, 4 s between requests.
- Instagram: headless Chromium, logged out, `og:description` per post, 4.5 s dwell + 2.5 s between
  requests, 12 posts per account, 6 accounts.
- Facebook Ad Library: headless Chromium, logged out, `active_status=active&country=US`, 9 s dwell,
  5-6 modest scrolls, 6 s between queries; 16 keyword queries.
- All scratch files under the session scratchpad. Nothing written to the repo except this document.
